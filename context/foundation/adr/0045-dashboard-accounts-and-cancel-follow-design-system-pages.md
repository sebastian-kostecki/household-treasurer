# Dashboard, Accounts and "Anuluj" follow the design system pages

Where an ADR departed from the "C · Atrament i morski" design system (artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/9gJSdtfpFgUAa8oEMgR4HA), the app goes back to the system: the pages `PageDashboard` and `PageAccounts` and the components `Button`, `EditBar` and `Notice` are the reference, as they stand on 2026-11-06. The reason is one look across the app. Each departure was reasonable on its own, but together they made the app drift from the system it had chosen, and an agent reading the system and the app saw two different answers.

Dashboard, budget execution. The metrics panel has three measures: To Spend of the current month with "Plan miesiąca →", Spendable Liquidity and Total funds. Year Elapsed leaves the panel. The left column is one panel "Budżet <year>" with the Switch "Postęp roku" and "Otwórz budżet" in its header, the plan measures (income, expenses, Planned Balance) and two tables, Dochody and Wydatki, with the columns Kategoria | Plan bieżący | Realizacja | Pozostało | Wykonanie and a sum row ("Suma dochodów", "Suma wydatków"). There are no Wszystko / Dochody / Wydatki tabs, no group rows and no Plan and Status columns; an exceeded Category still has the word "przekroczono" next to its percentage, because colour alone does not carry the status (ADR-0037). The Switch shows or hides the Year Elapsed mark on the meters that may carry it, and is remembered in the browser under `dashboard-year-elapsed`, with reads and writes inside try/catch. `context/foundation/glossary.md` carries the rule.

Dashboard, notices. The attention zone has the pills "Przekroczone · n" and "Ostrzeżenia · n" above the cards. A card is a link to its cause: the icon tile in the severity colour, the kind label on top ("Ponad limit", "Tempo przed upływem dni"), the identity in medium weight, and the value on the right in the severity colour. The payload, kinds, severities, order, links and the six-card limit of ADR-0032 do not change.

Accounts. The Switch "Pokaż zarchiwizowane" stands in the title row, next to "Dodaj konto". The bank tile shows a short text mark of the bank (`mB`, `PKO`, `ING`) instead of the logo, and a cash Account shows "zł". The short mark is a field of the bank list and is set in the implementing change. The table, the "zł" in each balance and the archived row stay as in ADR-0044.

"Anuluj" is `ghost` in every dialog, in the edit bar and in the Month Plan in change mode, as in `Button/README.md`. `outline` stays for secondary actions that are not a way out, such as "Importuj".

This supersedes in part ADR-0040 ("Anuluj" is `outline`), ADR-0043 (four measures with Year Elapsed, one execution panel with tabs, group rows and the Plan and Status columns, the mark with no toggle, and the notice card of its "Aktualizacja") and ADR-0044 (the Switch under the panel and the logo in the bank tile). The panels Konta and Lokaty of ADR-0043 and the rest of ADR-0044 stay. We rejected updating the system pages to match the app (the system would stop being a reference and follow each view instead) and reverting only the look while keeping the tabs and the four measures (the page would still differ from `PageDashboard` in structure). Completed by ADR-0050: the notice labels and values, the layout of the "Budżet <year>" block and the Switch state.
