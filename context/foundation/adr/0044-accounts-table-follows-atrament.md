# Accounts table follows Atrament, with "zł" in each balance

The Accounts view follows the "C · Atrament i morski" system (artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/9gJSdtfpFgUAa8oEMgR4HA, the `PageAccounts` pattern), like the Budget (ADR-0037), Operations (ADR-0038, ADR-0039), the Month Plan (ADR-0042) and the Dashboard (ADR-0043). The page order is: title row ("Konta", a description and "Dodaj konto"), one metrics panel, the `Switch` "Pokaż zarchiwizowane" and the table.

The table has the columns Nazwa | Typ | Saldo bieżące | Akcje, with a header row in the `table-header` style on the `.accounts-grid` class. Each balance goes through `formatMoney`, so it carries "zł", and the panel has no "Kwoty w zł" footer. This is the second exception to ADR-0037, next to the Operations list (ADR-0039), and it supersedes ADR-0037 only for the unit in the Accounts table. The reason is that the table has one column of amounts and no sum row, the sums stand in the metrics panel above, and the mockup does the same. The Budget and the Month Plan keep the unit in the footer.

An archived Account is `muted-foreground` in the whole row, without `opacity`, so the restore icon does not look disabled. Its negative balance stays `text-destructive` (FR-ACC-10), because Spendable Liquidity still counts it and the requirement says to mark it unambiguously. The FR-TD-08 exception of `design-system.md` therefore covers the balances of Term Deposits and Accounts.

The metrics panel is a `StatPanel` with two measures: Spendable Liquidity and Total funds, the latter captioned "z lokatami". The `Switch` stands in its own row under the panel, as in the Budget, because the title row holds one `primary` action only. The bank mark is the logo in `AccountMarkCompact` at 28 px; the abbreviations of the mockup are values from the preview, not from the repo, so they do not come in. The Account dialog takes the whole of ADR-0040 (`FormField`, a spinner, closing blocked while saving), with "Typ" as a `SegmentedControl` `block`.

We rejected the "Kwoty w zł" footer (ADR-0037; one column of amounts does not need a unit line), the `Switch` in the title row (a second control beside the only `primary` action), a caption with the amount of Term Deposits under Total funds (a second number, and a new prop), bank abbreviations in the tile (every named bank has a logo) and a muted tone for the negative balance of an archived Account (it would hide a reading that Spendable Liquidity still counts). The change touches no rule in `context/foundation/glossary.md` and no _Avoid_ term.
