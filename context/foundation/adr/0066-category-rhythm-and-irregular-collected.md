# A Category has a Rhythm; an Irregular expense has a rate and a Collected reading

Two independent flags on the Budget Line (ADR-0063) steer the Month Plan: Month Item Kind (Bill or Running) steers the month, the Paced / One-off flag steers the year. An expense paid several times a year fits neither: as Running it lowers To Spend in one jump in the month of the bill, as One-off it disappears from the months without a payment (PRD v3, Business Logic Changes). PRD v3 (structural decisions 2, 3, 5–9, FR-007, FR-009–FR-013, FR-022) replaces both flags with a Rhythm. This ADR changes clauses of ADR-0024, ADR-0030, ADR-0033, ADR-0043, ADR-0050, ADR-0062 and ADR-0063 as set out below. It changes no stored data: the migration, the history table, the endpoints and the snapshot version belong to S-24 and S-26, the 31 December step to S-27, merging Categories (FR-023) to S-21, and groups in the Month Plan to S-25.

Rhythms. An expense Category has one of four Rhythms. Polish UI copy: „stała opłata” (Fixed Charge), „bieżąca” (Running), „równomierna roczna” (Even Annual), „nieregularna” (Irregular). An income Category has no Rhythm and is read as today (plan, then the actual amount after the first inflow in the month). The rate of an Irregular item is „stawka” (Irregular Rate), the running total „zebrane” (Collected), the amount typed when marking „zebrane startowe” (Opening Collected). „Odkładam” stays only for Set-aside (FR-013). What each Rhythm does in each reading:

| Reading | Fixed Charge | Running | Even Annual | Irregular |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| To Spend | plan, then the actual amount after the first operation with a positive amount | the greater of plan and actual | the greater of plan and actual | Irregular Rate + surplus |
| Proposal | last paid amount | copy of the previous month (ADR-0033) | floor(Current Plan / 12), the remainder in December | as Even Annual (this is the Irregular Rate) |
| Over plan (`exceeded`) | actual > plan of the month | actual > plan of the month | Execution of the year > Current Plan (replaces `one_off_over_limit`) | surplus > 0 |
| Month Pace | no | yes (the only pool) | no | no |
| Month Remaining (past months without a Month Plan only) | in the pool | in the pool | outside the pool | outside the pool |
| Year Elapsed mark | yes | yes | yes | no |
| „W roku … z …” | as today (the To Spend rule) | as today | as today | Execution before the month + the actual amount of the month |
| Deviation in the Month Summary | actual − plan (unpaid: −plan in the sum) | actual − plan | actual − plan | surplus |

Even Annual shows beside the Proposal „zostało X na Y mies.”, where X is the Available Limit (Current Plan − Execution of the year) and Y the number of months to the end of the year including the current one (FR-009). The Month Remaining pool „Fixed Charge + Running” equals today’s Paced pool on all data without a One-off Category that is a bill (the dev data has none; S-24 counts such items on the production copy as a control number). In the Month Summary the Irregular row shows the actual bill, the rate and Collected at the end of the month; Month Result does not change; the totals deviation stays the plain sums (Execution − Current Month Plan, as today for an unpaid bill), and only the row shows the surplus. To Spend stays: income − items by Rhythm − Set-aside. The warning on an operation and in Import (To Spend falling below 0) uses the same rule, so a bill covered by Collected gives no warning.

Where the Rhythm lives and from when. A Rhythm is a history of entries (Rhythm, start month) on the list Category (ADR-0063), not a flag on the Budget Line and not a field of the Month Plan item: a Proposal is not stored (`month_plans.approved_at` is NOT NULL), so a past month without an approval has no item to carry it. The Rhythm of month m is the entry with the latest start month not after m; a month before the first entry has the Rhythm of the first entry (an Import operation in a past month of a new Category). A change in „Kategorie” replaces all entries from the current calendar month with one entry from that month. Past months do not change, and neither do the approved amounts of the current month: only the rule that reads them changes. Marking a Category Irregular writes Opening Collected on the entry (≥ 0, default 0); it can be corrected on an existing entry without changing the month of marking, and the chain recomputes itself. Removing „nieregularna” ends Collected: from that month there is no reading and no balance moves. Marking again starts anew, with a new month of marking and a new Opening Collected. Saving the Rhythm the Category already has adds no entry; correcting Opening Collected edits the entry that started the current Irregular period and never adds one; removing and marking again within one month replaces that month’s entry, so the new period starts in that month with the new Opening Collected. Identical consecutive entries merge only in the migration. A new expense Category is Running from the current calendar month. Example: „Prąd” marked on 9 November 2026 with Opening Collected 300 is Irregular from November; the rate of November is its amount in the approved plan of November; October and earlier do not change.

Irregular Rate. The rate is the amount of the item in the Month Plan of the month. A Proposal writes floor(Current Plan / 12) of its Budget Line, December takes the remainder (Current Plan − 11 × floor), as the average of ADR-0033. Approving freezes the amount in the Approved Month Plan, and in change mode it can be corrected (Current Month Plan). The rate of a month without an approved plan, past or current, is the Proposal formula (floor(Current Plan / 12), December the remainder) computed from the present Current Plan; a past month has no Proposal reading (`MonthPlanProposal` returns none for it), so the rate comes from the formula, not from that reading. A later change of the plan therefore also changes the rate of past unapproved months and Collected at the start of the following months, and with it the surplus and To Spend of even an approved month (December unapproved, January approved), as a late operation does (FR-011); the Dashboard warns about a missing approval from the 3rd day. A month in which the year’s Budget is not in execution has rate 0; the phase is read when Collected is read, so starting the Budget later gives its unapproved months their rate afterwards. Archiving the Budget Line drops it from Proposals of the months that start after archival (ADR-0063, ADR-0033): in those months without an approved plan the rate is 0 and Collected stands; an approved plan of such a month has no item for it, so its rate is 0 too. The archive state is read when Collected is read, so restoring gives the unapproved months of the archived period their rate back and resumes the rate. What happens to Collected on a merge (FR-023) S-21 decides. Example: „Prąd” Irregular from January 2027, Opening Collected 0, plan 1 800, January–June approved, bills of 400 in March and in May, and in July the annual plan raised to 2 400. The rates January–June stay 150 each, Collected on 1 July is 150 (May: 200 + 150 − 400 leaves a surplus of 50 that lowers To Spend of May), and from July the Proposal writes 200 (December: 2 400 − 11 × 200 = 200). To Spend of January–June does not change. The rates of the year sum to 6 × 150 + 5 × 200 + 200 = 2 100 of 2 400: after a mid-year plan change the rates do not add up to the Current Plan, and the remainder in December only evens out rounding. The missing 300 appears as a surplus in the month of a bill, unless the user raises the rates in change mode (PRD, structural decision 9: the user corrects the plan).

Collected. A reading, never stored: Collected at the start of the month of marking is Opening Collected, and Collected at the end of a month m is max(0, Collected at the start of the month + Irregular Rate − actual amount), where the actual amount is the Execution of the Category from operations dated in the month (refunds net), and the end of m is the start of m + 1. The surplus of m is max(0, actual amount − (Collected at the start of the month + Irregular Rate)). The floor at 0 applies in every month, not only at the end of the chain, because a surplus has already lowered To Spend of its own month and cannot be repaid from later rates; this refines the single „not less than 0” sum of PRD v3 (Business Logic Changes), which in the July example would give 100 instead of 150 on 1 July. A late operation or a correction recomputes Collected by itself (FR-011). US-02: on 1 July Collected 300, rate 150, a bill of 400 → Collected 50, surplus 0, the item counts −150 in To Spend (rate 150 + surplus 0); a bill of 600 → Collected 0, surplus 150, the item counts −300 in To Spend, an overrun, and Month Result counts 400 or 600. A refund of −50 in August, with Collected 50 at the start of August and rate 150 → Collected at the end of August 50 + 150 + 50 = 250. Collected does not move Account balances, Spendable Liquidity, Total funds, Month Result or Execution, and it is not a transfer, an account, a Reserve or an Earmark. A Collected reading beside the balances is not added (PRD, structural decision 6).

31 December. The chain does not stop at the end of the year: Collected on 1 January is Collected at the end of December, and the rate of January comes from the plan of the new year. „Zebrane startowe” of a new Budget in the UI (FR-012) is this reading, not a stored number, because the 2027 Budget is created in December. Example: „Prąd” with Collected 200 on 31 December 2026 and a 2027 plan of 2 100 → on 1 January 2027 Collected 200, rate of January 175 (2 100 / 12). A 2027 Budget still in draft on 15 January 2027 has rate 0 for January, and Collected from 31 December stands.

Fixed Charge Proposal. If the previous month had a first operation with a positive amount on the Category and its actual amount (refunds net) is > 0, the Proposal writes that last paid amount. Otherwise (also a payment of 100 and a refund of 150, net −50) it writes the plan of the previous month, and with no source the average of ADR-0033. January reads December of the same list Category (ADR-0063). Example: „Telefon + Internet” with the plan of October 180 and a payment of 172.15 → the Proposal for November is 172.15; October without a payment → 180. Two payments in the previous month give their net sum. Running keeps copying the plan of the previous month.

Migration and snapshot. Every expense Budget Line gets a history entry from January of the year of its Budget with the Rhythm from the flags; identical consecutive entries merge. An income Category has no entry.

| `is_paced` | `month_item_kind` | Rhythm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| true | running | Running |
| false | running | Even Annual |
| true or false | bill | Fixed Charge |

No Category becomes Irregular in the migration (PRD). A snapshot of version 1 to 8 (ADR-0070) goes through the same mapping on restore, after the steps of ADR-0063 (FR-022); a new snapshot version carries the history with Opening Collected (S-24, S-26). A new Category is Running from the current month. The flags stay on the Budget Line until S-24 moves their readers (`.ai/rules/migrations.md`), so no month’s To Spend moves in the migration. Dev example (user 1, Budget 2026): 31 expenses, 29 „Paced + Running” → Running, and „Ubezpieczenie samochodu” (plan 541 zł) and „Rekolekcje” (plan 2 380 zł), „One-off + Running” → Even Annual. To Spend of every approved month stays to the grosz, because Running and Even Annual both take the greater of plan and actual and Fixed Charge keeps the Bill rule. From the first Proposal after S-24, „Rekolekcje” proposes 198.33 zł (December 198.37 zł) and „Ubezpieczenie samochodu” 45.08 zł (December 45.12 zł), until the user marks them Irregular (FR-007); both get the Year Elapsed mark they do not have as One-off.

New wording of clauses:

- ADR-0024: the Month Remaining pool is Fixed Charge + Running in that month; „a One-off Category has no Month Remaining” becomes „Even Annual and Irregular have no Month Remaining”, and the Rhythm is a history on the list Category, not a flag copied onto a new Budget.
- ADR-0033: the Proposal follows the Rhythm (table), Fixed Charge proposes the last paid amount, Month Item Kind is no longer copied onto a new Budget, To Spend is by Rhythm (Bill items and Running items above become the table), and „A One-off Category always starts at 0” becomes the Even Annual and Irregular average. The rejected „irregular-expense fund” stays rejected: Collected is a reading in the plan, not a fund with transfers. Nor is it the rejected „carrying the remainder forward”: Collected chains only the Irregular Rate of one Irregular Category, with a floor at 0 in each month, and never carries a month’s leftover, Set-aside or Draw.
- ADR-0030: an Earmark comes from positive leftover on any expense Category, instead of „(Paced or One-off)”.
- ADR-0043: the Year Elapsed mark stands on income and on expense Categories other than Irregular, instead of „income and Paced expense Categories, not One-off”.
- ADR-0050: the `one_off_over_limit` notice (Ponad limit) becomes the over-plan signal of an Even Annual Category against the year (Execution of the year > Current Plan); an Irregular Category raises `month_item_over_plan` on a surplus.
- ADR-0062: the automatic raise on entering change mode and „Ustaw plan na wydatki” do not apply to an Irregular item, as they would raise the rate to the amount of the bill; other Rhythms are unchanged.
- ADR-0063: the Rhythm is an attribute of the list Category with a history, not a flag on the Budget Line; „the Paced / One-off flag and the Month Item Kind stay on the Budget Line” holds only until S-24, and the January Proposal clause on Month Item Kind is void.

We rejected:

- the Rhythm on the Budget Line (no granularity of a month, and edited per year instead of in „Kategorie”);
- the Rhythm stored on the Month Plan item (Proposals are not stored, and past months without a plan have none);
- a change from the next month (marking in November would work from December);
- the rate always as Current Plan / 12, approved months included (a plan change would recompute approved past months and create Collected with no money; only past months without an approval follow the present plan), or fixed until January (raising the plan would have no effect);
- the rate as (Current Plan − rates of earlier months) / remaining months (leaves „plan / 12” of the PRD, needs a second chain and a rule for a plan lowered below the rates already collected);
- Collected as a stored number, or kept after „nieregularna” is removed (every correction would need a rewrite);
- Collected without the floor in each month (a surplus would be counted twice);
- all expenses Even Annual (Month Pace disappears) and One-off → Running (the amount returns in the Proposal and enters the pace);
- Irregular by default in the migration (PRD);
- „odkładam” for the rate (two different „odkładam”, in the plan and in the Summary);
- the Fixed Charge Proposal as a copy of the plan (identical to Running);
- a fund with transfers to a separate account (PRD v1, ADR-0033; PRD v3: the money stays on the main account).
