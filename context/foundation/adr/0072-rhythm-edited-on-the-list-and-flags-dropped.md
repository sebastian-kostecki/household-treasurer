# The Rhythm is edited only in „Kategorie”; the flags leave the database in S-24

ADR-0066 replaces the Paced / One-off flag and Month Item Kind with a Rhythm kept as a history on the list Category, but leaves open who edits it, what the Month Plan shows instead of the old switch, how the Even Annual reading looks in the plan, what the migration does with a Category that has no Budget Line, what the write of an entry looks like, and when the flags are removed. S-24 (FR-007, FR-009, FR-022) needs these answered before the code. This ADR adds to ADR-0066 and changes ADR-0033 in the part about the switch; it does not touch ADR-0062. It also corrects one scope: the snapshot mapping of ADR-0066 covers versions 1 to 8, not 1 to 6, because versions 6 to 8 are also formats without a Rhythm.

Who edits the Rhythm. The Rhythm changes only in „Kategorie”, in the create and edit window of an expense Category (three values until S-26: Fixed Charge, Running, Even Annual). The Month Plan and the Budget page show the Rhythm as a label. The Bill / Running switch in the Month Plan row (`budgets/{budget}/categories/{category}/month-item-kind`) and the flag fields in the Budget window go away without a replacement. The switch rewrote the kind for the whole year, past months included; the history of ADR-0066 does not.

Write rule. One Action serves creating, editing and changing the type. If the Rhythm of the current month (read rule below) equals the saved one and the Category has no entry starting after the current month, nothing happens. Otherwise (also the same Rhythm when a future entry exists, for example January 2027 from the migration of a draft with other flags: the list shows only the current month, so a save means „so from now on”) the Action deletes every entry with a start month not before the current month, future ones included, and inserts one entry from the current month. Identical consecutive entries are not merged by a write (only the migration merges, ADR-0066). Changing the type to income deletes all entries; changing it to expense inserts an entry from the current month with the chosen Rhythm (Running by default). The window says „Zmiana działa od <current month>; minione miesiące bez zmian”.

Read rule. The Rhythm of month m is the entry with the latest start month not after m; a month before the first entry has the Rhythm of the first entry. An expense Category with no entry (an invariant violation, covered by a test) reads as Running; an income Category has no Rhythm. Every reader takes the Rhythm of the month it is about: the Month Plan and its Proposal the viewed month; To Spend, Month Pace, notices and the Year Elapsed mark on the Dashboard the current month; the Budget page the viewed month; hand entry and Import the month of the operation date; „Kategorie” the current month. One class (`App\Support\CategoryRhythms`) loads the entries of a set of Categories in one query and answers both questions, for a month and for a whole year.

Even Annual in the Month Plan. In the current and in future months of the Month Plan, in a Proposal and after approval, the row of an Even Annual item shows „zostało X na Y mies.” as its subtitle instead of „Pozostały limit roczny”: X is the Current Plan minus the Execution of the year as of today, Y is the number of calendar months from the current one to December, the current one included. In the current month the text is appended to the line „W roku”. Past months of the plan, the Budget page and the Dashboard do not show it. The collapsed section „Jednorazowe bez planu i wydatków” is renamed „Równomierne roczne bez planu i wydatków” and takes the rule of the One-off item (amount 0 and no operation in the month) over to Even Annual items; other Rhythms have no collapsed section. `one_off_over_limit` is renamed `even_annual_over_limit` (ADR-0066), and an Even Annual item does not raise `month_item_over_plan`.

Fixed Charge from an unapproved past month. The Proposal of a Fixed Charge in a month whose source month is past and has no approved plan: a payment in that month wins over the average of ADR-0033 (the last paid amount, ADR-0066); with no payment and no source, the average.

Migration. Every expense Budget Line gives an entry from January of the year of its Budget by the table of ADR-0066 (a bill is Fixed Charge whatever the Paced flag says); identical consecutive entries merge. An expense Category with no Budget Line at all gets Running from the month of the migration. The migration changes no row of `budget_lines`, `budgets`, operations or plans. The mapping is a frozen class of plain arrays (`App\Support\CategoryList\LegacyRhythmConversion`) shared by the migration and by the restore of snapshots 1 to 8, so the two cannot disagree; like `LegacyCategoryConversion` it is not edited after release. `app:control-numbers` reports the count of One-off + Bill lines (`meta.one_off_bill_lines`) and the count of history rows (`meta.category_rhythm_rows`) under `meta`, which `differences()` ignores.

Snapshot. Version 9 carries the history on the Category; versions 1 to 8 go through the mapping above on restore.

Dropping the flags. S-24 removes `is_paced`, `month_item_kind` and the `MonthItemKind` enum after the last reader has moved (expand, backfill, switch the readers, drop; `.ai/rules/migrations.md`). The `down()` of the dropping migration restores the flags from the Rhythm: for every expense Budget Line the Rhythm of its year in the current month (the current year), in December (a past year) or in January (a future year), Fixed Charge = (paced, bill), Running = (paced, running), Even Annual = (one-off, running); an income line = (paced, running). The restore is lossy and the test says so: a Rhythm changed in the middle of a year returns as one flag for the whole year, and a One-off + Bill line becomes Fixed Charge in the migration and returns as paced + bill, so its One-off flag is lost. Data lost after the drop (a Rhythm change made in „Kategorie”) is the reason for the backup before every production migration (ADR-0064).

We rejected:

- a Rhythm switch in the Month Plan row (a second place to edit, and the history of ADR-0066 would need a month picker there);
- no Rhythm column in the Month Plan row (the user would not see which rule drives the amount);
- reading the Rhythm only for the Proposal, or only in the current month (To Spend, Month Pace and the notices of a past month would change when the Rhythm is changed);
- a collapsed section for every Rhythm, or removing the section (a Running item with no plan is still a normal row; an unused Even Annual item is the case the section was made for);
- leaving the flags for a separate change (two sources of truth with writers for both);
- a `down()` that throws (the migration would not be reversible on a deploy rollback; a lossy restore with a documented rule is better than none);
- merging identical entries on a write (a user's explicit save is a statement „from now on”, and the migration is the only place with history to compact).
