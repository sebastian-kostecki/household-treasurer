# Merging two Categories folds the source into the target; a server preview comes first and a snapshot before the write

ADR-0063 gives the Category to the User for all years and ADR-0066 leaves open „what happens to Collected on a merge (FR-023)”. ADR-0063 and ADR-0067 say that „Usuń” refuses a Category with history and that the way out is archive „or merge (S-21)”. S-21 (FR-023) is that merge: the User folds two Categories of the list into one, to clean up typos and test data, before the Import accuracy is measured in S-23. The merge cannot be undone in the application, so the numbers come first, and the rules below must be fixed before the code. This ADR adds to ADR-0063, ADR-0066, ADR-0067 and ADR-0073 and changes the two clauses named under „Immutable plans”. Merging more than two Categories at once and splitting a Category stay in the roadmap’s Parked list.

Who with whom. The same User, the same type, two different Categories. The target must be active; the source may be active or archived. A merge into an archived target is refused with „Restore the target category before merging.”, because a merge that also restored the target would do two things in one click. Name, icon, colour, Category Group, description and order of the target stay; so does its Rhythm history (below). The User who wants the name or icon of the source edits the target before or after the merge on „Kategorie”.

Budget Lines. For every Budget in which the source has a Budget Line: if the target has none there, the Budget Line of the source is re-pointed to the target (same id, so its Financial Operations and Month Plan items are not touched); if both have one, the two fold into the Budget Line of the target: Initial Plan, Current Plan and Execution add up, the Financial Operations move to it, the archive state of the target’s line stays, and the source’s line is deleted. Totals of every Budget are the same before and after. Example (the pair on production): „Jedzenie poza domem” has a Budget Line in 2026 with 48 operations, „Restauracje” only in the 2027 draft; the merge re-points the 2026 line to „Restauracje”, nothing is added up, and the operations, the plan and Execution of 2026 stay exactly where they were. Example (one Budget): „Jedzenie poza domem” with Current Plan 3 000 and „Restauracje” with 1 500 in the same Budget fold into one line of 4 500, with the Initial Plans and the Executions added the same way.

Immutable plans. The glossary says the Initial Plan is „immutable afterwards” and the Approved Month Plan is „immutable” (ADR-0033, ADR-0063). A merge is the one exception: where two lines fold, the Initial Plan of the target becomes the sum of both, and where both Categories have an item in the same Month Plan, the approved amount and the current amount of the target’s item become the sums. Where only the source has an item, the item is re-pointed to the target’s Budget Line unchanged. The Budget total, the Month Plan total and every Execution do not change, which is why this is not an edit of a plan but a re-labelling of the same money; the control sums below enforce it.

Archived source. An archived source merges as if it had been restored first: its Budget Line in the Budget in execution of the current calendar year, when re-pointed, stops being archived (ADR-0063 archives exactly that line), and a re-pointed line of an older Budget keeps its `archived_at`. Where the source folds into an active target line, the state of the target stays. Example: „Wakacje” (archived 2026-09-09) merged into „Urlop” re-points its 2026 line to „Urlop” and clears `archived_at`, so the 2026 totals of the Budget now include it (the preview says so in a note); its 2025 line keeps the archive date.

Rhythm. The Rhythm history of the target stays; the history of the source is deleted with the source. Past months of the source’s operations are read with the target’s Rhythm afterwards, and every reading that depends on them (Collected, To Spend, the surplus) recomputes itself, as it does after a late operation (ADR-0066, FR-011). The write rule stays: nothing writes a Rhythm entry except `ChangeCategoryRhythm` (ADR-0072), and the merge calls a new method of it.

Collected. When the target is Irregular in the current month, the merge starts a new period on it from the current month, with Opening Collected equal to the sum of Collected of both Categories on the first day of the current month (the source counts as 0 when it is not Irregular then). The method deletes the entries starting in the current month or later and inserts one Irregular entry from the current month (ADR-0072 write rule, ADR-0073 storage); earlier entries stay, so the past period ends the month before. When the target is not Irregular, the Collected of the source disappears, and the preview warns with the amount when it is above 0. Example: „Energia” Irregular with Collected 400 and „Prąd” Irregular with 450 on 1 November merge into „Prąd” with a new period from November and Opening Collected 850; its January entry stays. The reading on the first day of the month is what the preview and the new entry carry, and the plan row shows Collected at the end of the month (ADR-0073), so the two differ by the rate and the bills of the current month, which is intended: the Collected at the end of the current month after the merge equals the sum of the two readings from before it, when neither hits the floor at 0.

Preview. Before confirming, the window shows what will happen, computed on the server by the same class that the action uses, so the preview and the write cannot disagree. One row per Budget in which the source has a Budget Line: the year (with „szkic” for a draft), the number of operations and the Current Plan of the target „before → after”. Under the table: the number and range of Month Plan items that move, the Collected line, the warning about lost Collected, and the note that an archived line returns to the Budget totals. A sentence that the merge cannot be undone closes it. The „Scal” button stays disabled until the preview of the chosen target has loaded.

Safety copy and control sums. The action saves the snapshot of the User (`BackupUserData`) after taking its locks and before the first write, and merges nothing when that fails: the failure is reported and returned to the window as an error. The way back is `user:restore` from that file, which also undoes everything entered after the merge and gives the records new ids; the application logs the path on success, since that is the only place the User finds the right file. The snapshot format stays at version 10 and nothing is migrated. In the same transaction the action compares, before and after, the sums of Initial Plan, Current Plan and Execution and the number of operations per Budget, and the sums of the approved and current amounts per Month Plan; a difference throws and rolls back.

Writes. The order is forced by the foreign keys: a Financial Operation blocks the deletion of its Budget Line (ADR-0065), so the operations move first, then the Month Plan items, then the sums on the target’s line, then the folded source lines are deleted, the remaining lines are re-pointed, the new Collected period is written, and last the source Category is deleted (its Rhythm history goes with it by cascade). The action runs under the lock of the whole list of the type (`CategoryListStore::state`), and Collected is computed under that lock from the data before any write.

Interface. Every row of „Kategorie”, active or archived, gets a „⋯” menu with „Scal z inną…” and, where deleting is allowed, „Usuń”; edit and archive stay icons. „Scal z inną…” opens one window per page, built on `ConfirmActionDialog` (destructive), with the source in the title and a selector of the active Categories of the same type. „Kategorie” still shows no amounts; amounts appear only in the window. Cash Expectation, Earmark and Reserve do not exist in the code; the change that adds any of them adds its re-pointing to the merge, its control sum and a test (`.ai/rules/categories.md`).

Relation to earlier rules. „Usuń” keeps refusing a Category with a Financial Operation, a Cash Expectation or a Budget Line in a Budget in execution (ADR-0063, ADR-0067): the second way out, next to archive, is now the merge. The Rhythm and Collected rules of ADR-0066 and ADR-0073 are unchanged except for the new period above.

We rejected:

- the plan of the target winning, and the plan of the source being dropped (money would disappear from the Budget totals);
- refusing a merge when both Categories have a line in one Budget (it is the case the User needs for typos made in one year);
- recomputing the Collected chain over the merged operations alone (the rates and the Opening Collected of the source would be lost, and the result would differ from the sum the User saw);
- refusing an Irregular source (the User would have to remove the Rhythm first and lose Collected);
- merging into an archived target, and offering only active sources (restoring and merging are two decisions);
- a fourth icon in the row, and a button in the section header (the row has no room in 7 rem, and the action belongs to one row);
- a one-sentence preview, and the full breakdown of Initial Plan, Execution and each month (a table per Budget answers „what happens to my numbers” without hiding the money in a wall of figures);
- confirming by retyping the name (the preview and the snapshot are the protection);
- a merge log and undo in the application (`user:restore` from the snapshot is the way back, and the merge is a rare clean-up).
