# Operation dialogs show a before → after preview block and follow the Atrament field order

The four operation dialogs (expense, income, own transfer, capital movement) follow the "C · Atrament i morski" system README (artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/9gJSdtfpFgUAa8oEMgR4HA, component Dialog and its `preview.html`), on top of the shell, field and focus rules of ADR-0040.

The dialog has a preview block (`.form-preview`, `background`, `radius-md`) with rows "label: before → after": To Spend in every affected month that has a plan (an edit that moves the date between months gives up to two rows, ascending), the annual limit of the selected category (an expense or a refund), and the balance of the selected account or term deposit (the source account in an own transfer). "Before" is the saved state: the limit "before" is plan − execution of the selected category with no correction for the record being edited, and the balance "before" is `current_balance_minor_units`. A row appears only when its reading is known, and the block renders when the amount is valid and greater than 0 and there is at least one row or warning. Values are coloured by `amountToneClass`, a deviation from `foreground` in `Dialog/preview.html`, as `design-system.md` ("Kolor kwoty ze znakiem") requires one recipe for a signed amount colour. Non-blocking warnings are `warning` text inside the block, under the reading they concern; warnings with no reading (Month Remaining) come at the end of the block. This replaces `Alert` `warning` in form dialogs; the Import preview and other views stay with `Alert` and `bg-warning/10`. The month label is in the locative case with the year ("w listopadzie 2026").

The field order follows Atrament, with "Kwota | Data" in one row (`.form-field-row`, two equal columns, one column in a window narrower than 390px). A wrong amount is unreadable or negative; zero is still rejected by the server, and the field keeps its error from the server. A choice of type in the expense and the income dialog is `SegmentedControl` `block` in the tone `primary` with no `name` when the payload is a conversion flag (`convert_to_income`, `convert_to_expense_refund` in a hidden field); the capital movement direction uses `name="direction"`. This is a deviation from the sentence "sent with the form through `name`" in ADR-0040. The statement description is a text block (`.form-readonly-text`, `muted`, `radius-md`), not a `textarea readonly`.

This supersedes in part the sentence in `context/foundation/design-system.md` that sends every non-blocking form warning to `Alert` `warning`, and the matching rule in `.ai/rules/js.md`, for form dialogs only. We rejected an `Alert` beside the block (two places for one warning), a "before" without the edited operation (the number would not match the saved state), a month label in the nominative (it does not read as a sentence) and a locative without the year (ambiguous across years), a "field touched" state (the message waits for an edit of the field), and one "Nowa operacja" dialog (the four operations differ in fields and readings).
