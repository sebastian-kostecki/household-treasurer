# A Month Plan sits beside the annual Budget; To Spend replaces Month Remaining where a plan exists

This supersedes only the clause of ADR-0024 that rejected “a second monthly Budget”; the rest of ADR-0024 (Available Limit stays annual Current Plan minus booked Execution, the Month Remaining arithmetic) stands. A Budget in execution gets, beside its annual plan, a Month Plan for each current and future calendar month: one calendar month, items are the Categories of that Budget with amounts in Minor units, plus one signed Set-aside amount. Past months stay without a Month Plan. A Month Plan is either a Proposal (not approved) or Approved; once approved, its Approved Month Plan amounts never change when the annual Current Plan changes, and its Current Month Plan amounts stay editable through the month. The annual Budget works unchanged; the sum of the Month Plans may differ from the year, and that gap stays visible rather than being forced to zero.

A Proposal copies the amounts of the Month Plan of the previous calendar month in any state (Approved → its Current Month Plan, Proposal → that Proposal’s amounts); January reads December of the previous Budget for a Category with the same name and type (a lookup, not a shared identity). With no source, the amount is the annual average: floor(Current Plan / 12) of that Category in Minor units, with December taking the remainder (Current Plan − 11 × floor), as the draft reading in `MonthRemainingCalculator.php:81-90` does; this is a Proposal amount, not Month Remaining. The Set-aside of a Proposal copies the positive Set-aside of the same source, and a Draw or no source gives 0 (settled by S-02). A One-off Category always starts at 0, a Category added mid-year joins at 0, and an archived Category stays in the month where it has a plan or an expense and drops out of later Proposals. Month Item Kind (Bill or Running) is copied onto a new Budget like the Paced / One-off flag (ADR-0024:3, `app/Actions/CreateBudget.php:44`; ADR-0014 does not mention that flag).

To Spend is a signed, unstored reading: income (plan, then the actual amount after the first inflow in the month) − Bill items (plan, then the actual amount after the first expense with a positive amount in the month) − Running items (the greater of plan and actual) − Set-aside; a positive Set-aside lowers To Spend and a negative one (a Draw) raises it. “The month has a plan” includes a Proposal. Month Remaining stays only for past months without a Month Plan, so the `paced_month_remaining` Dashboard notice no longer applies once the current Budget is in execution, because Dashboard always shows the current calendar month (ADR-0031). Until S-06, S-07 and S-08 update them, these documents are overridden for months with a Month Plan: FR-TRX-08 and FR-REP-13 in `context/foundation/prd-mvp.md`, the Month Remaining tile and notice in `context/archive/2026-09-23-dashboard-hierarchy/spec.md`, the Month Remaining alert sentence in ADR-0031, and the “over Month Remaining” warning in `.ai/rules/js.md`.

Four Dashboard notices come from the Month Plan, weighted by the ADR-0032 definition (`exceeded` is a limit already broken, `warning` still within bounds): To Spend below 0 is an `exceeded` Dashboard notice; an item over plan (actual above plan, any size, including plan 0) is an `exceeded` Dashboard notice because the overrun is already a fact even though a Month Plan is soft; Month Pace at least 10 percentage points ahead of Month Elapsed is a `warning` Dashboard notice; no Approved Month Plan from the 3rd day of the month is a `warning` Dashboard notice (a Proposal always exists for the current month, so “no plan” literally never happens). Thresholds and definitions are in `context/foundation/glossary.md`.

We rejected the annual Budget with corrections (default amounts would be the average, an assumption), a full monthly Budget (rebuilds the annual Budget and needs a data migration), carrying the remainder forward (a chain of months, and double counting with Draw), closing a month, an irregular-expense fund, and Month Pace per Category (weekly shopping makes it noisy).

Superseded in part by ADR-0037: the Budget page no longer shows Month Result, Month Remaining or To Spend; the Period Report, the Dashboard and the Month Plan own those readings.
Amended by ADR-0063: January reads December of the same list Category (not a lookup by name and type), items are the Categories with a Budget Line on that Budget, and Month Item Kind comes from the previous year’s Budget Line instead of being copied with the Category.

Amended by ADR-0066: the Proposal follows the Rhythm (Fixed Charge proposes the last paid amount), Month Item Kind is no longer copied onto a new Budget, To Spend counts items by Rhythm instead of Bill and Running items, „A One-off Category always starts at 0” becomes the Even Annual and Irregular average, and an Irregular item has an Irregular Rate and Collected; the rejected irregular-expense fund and carrying the remainder forward stay rejected.

Amended by ADR-0070: the Month Item Kind switch in the Month Plan row is gone; the Rhythm is changed only in „Kategorie”, and the Month Plan shows it as a label.
