# Reports view: title row in every state, room for the chart, the share under the amount, a sink on the real page

The change `reports-view` (from the `/10x-ui` audit of `Reports.vue`) fixes five faults of the Reports view and its kitchen sink `/dev/ui/reports`: empty states without a title row (C1), async charts that arrive without a place and push the table down (C2, C4), a sink that keeps its own copy of the page (C3) and a share column that cuts category names on a phone (C5). This ADR records the four decisions before the code. It adds to ADR-0070, ADR-0071, ADR-0074 and ADR-0081; it changes one scope decision of S-32 (`context/archive/2026-11-12-rolling-twelve-month-report/plan.md:102`) and sharpens ADR-0081:7, and no clause of the other three.

Title row and switch. Every state of a User with a Budget starts with the title row: the `h1` with the title of the selected view and the period switch with its four segments (Rok, Miesiąc, 12 miesięcy, Trend). The empty states sit under it. A Budget in preparation shows the „Budżet w przygotowaniu” sheet under the row for the Year, the Month and the Trend (the server still sends `report: null` and `trend: null` there); „12 miesięcy” shows the window report, because ADR-0081 resolves the window before the phase of the selected Budget, and the switch leads back to the sheet, so no segment is a one-way street. This replaces the reading that the draft state never takes the view over, and makes „the segment is always available when the User has any Budget” (ADR-0081:7) true in the UI as well, not only by URL. A User without any Budget has nothing to switch, and last-12 does not work for them: the page shows the `h1` „Raporty” and the „Brak budżetu” sheet, with no switch.

Room for the chart. A chart loaded with `defineAsyncComponent` (ADR-0071) gets a `loadingComponent` with `delay: 0`, so that the placeholder stands from the first frame (with the default 200 ms a cached chunk arrives inside the delay and the jump stays). The placeholder is a `Skeleton` in a `ListSurface`, as high as the chart will be: the height of the bar chart is one pure function `reportChartHeightRem(barCount, compared)` in `@/lib/report-chart`, used by the chart and by the placeholder (the size constants of the chart move there, so they are no longer loose numbers in a component); the Trend chart keeps its fixed height. The legend is one component shared by the chart and the placeholder, so the placeholder reserves exactly its height. With no bars there is neither a chart nor a placeholder. The placeholders are imported statically and do not load Unovis, so ADR-0071 (Unovis off the other pages) holds. A chunk that fails to load goes to the Vue error handler as before; the table beside it carries the data.

Share under the amount. In a card narrower than 36rem (the width of the card, not of the window, like the comparison in ADR-0074) the share leaves its column and stands under the amount, as „udział 66,7%”: on the second line of the row without a comparison and on the third line with one (amount | previous year and difference | share). The name then keeps the whole width of the first column, and the badge „zarchiwizowana” wraps under the name when it does not fit. From 36rem the row is one line as before, with the „Udział” column and without the label. The category chip is not changed. In the grid below 36rem the amount column starts at 4.5rem (not the 5.5rem of the plan) and the share cell spans both columns, right-aligned: „udział 30,9%” alone in the amount column would widen it to about 94 px, so the name keeps about 200 px (176 px in a nested row). The reason is the measure: at 22rem the content is about 288 px and the three-column grid left the name about 96 px.

Sink on the real page. `/dev/ui/reports` renders `Reports.vue` itself in every scenario, with full `ReportsPageProps` from the fixture (`budgetYear` and `budgetPhase` included), as `/dev/ui/auth` renders the real entry pages (ADR-0056:37). A new state is a new scenario, not a new markup in the sink; the sink keeps the state matrix, the phone frame and a scenario `draft-last-12`. A click on the switch or on a month in the sink navigates to the real `/reports`, which we accept. Each embedded page sets `<Head>`, so the tab of the sink shows the title of Reports; the sink keeps its own `h1`. This ADR changes no word of `context/foundation/glossary.md` and no _Avoid_ term, changes no Inertia prop (the server and the `assertInertia` tests stay as they are) and adds no package.

We rejected:

- a switch with disabled segments in the draft (a disabled state against ADR-0081:7), a title alone with „12 miesięcy” reachable only by URL, and a second button in the draft sheet (two entries to one view);
- keeping the page without a title row for the empty states (the page cannot be recognised on 22rem, and the draft has no way back from last-12);
- extracting the body of the page into a new component for the sink to share (more moving, and the title would have to live inside it), and keeping the hand-made copies (they already drifted: no `range`, other titles);
- placeholder height as size tokens in `app.css` computed with `calc()` (the name width of 110 px for Unovis stays in JS anyway), and a fixed minimum height (the page would still jump by the difference);
- the placeholder with the default `delay` of 200 ms, and an `errorComponent` for a failed chunk (a loud placeholder for what the table already says);
- breaking the category name over lines (a new prop in the shared `CategoryChip`) and deferring the narrow layout;
- the share on the second line of the three columns, beside the previous year (it fits the grid, but the second line of a row with a comparison already holds two cells, so the share would have no place of its own);
- leaving the share unchanged when a comparison is shown (two layouts of one column below 36rem, and the name is cut in the compared table too).
