# One AccountAvatar with the bank logo for every Account and Term Deposit

ADR-0045, ADR-0047 and ADR-0051 replaced the bank logo with a short text mark everywhere except the Dashboard, so one Account looked different on each view: a logo on the Dashboard, a text tile in the tables and pick lists, and the name alone in the Operations rows, filters, chips and notices. The user recognises an Account by its bank logo. From now on there is one `AccountAvatar` for every Account and every Term Deposit shown as a row or an option, with the bank logo and a short mark as the fallback. It replaces `AccountMarkCompact`, `AccountIdentityBlock` and the tile of `CompactAccountIdentity`.

The scale has three sizes: `xs` 16 px, `sm` 20 px and `md` 28 px. `md` stands in the tables and rows: Dashboard Accounts and Term Deposits, the Accounts table, the Term Deposits table, and the own-transfer and capital-movement rows (Account and Term Deposit). `xs` stands where the avatar sits in a line of text: the Dashboard notices, the meta line of a financial operation row and the active filter chip. `sm` stands in the pick lists: the Account filter of Operations (in `AccountSelect`), `AccountSelect` in the dialogs and in import, the import pair candidate and `AccountBankSelect`. `AccountBankSelect` always shows the short mark and never the logo, because it picks a Bank, not an Account, and has no payload with URLs.

The avatar is a tile on `muted` with `radius-sm`, no border, and the logo is `object-contain`, so square, transparent and portrait files all fit. The tile is `aria-hidden` and the image has `alt=""`, because the name of the Account always stands next to it (ADR-0047); `alt` with the name made a screen reader read it twice.

The fallback is the short mark in the `mark-label` style, with the table of ADR-0047 unchanged. A cash Account shows "zł", also on the Dashboard, instead of `Coins`. An Account of the bank `other`, or with no bank, shows `Building2`. When the image fails to load, the avatar switches to the same short mark. A Term Deposit has a bank but no type, so it takes the bank part of the same rules.

ING, Pekao and Santander get no logo: `Bank::markPath()` returns `null` for them and the files are removed from `public/marks`, because they were three letters on a coloured square, not logos, and they would show a mark that differs from the short mark in size and font. These banks show the short mark. A bank gets its logo in `Bank::markPath()` in a separate change, and a new bank gets its short mark in `account-identity.ts` in the commit that adds it.

A select that shows an avatar is a reka `Select` (ADR-0045, `.ai/rules/js.md`): the Account filter of Operations and the import pair candidate become one. The Term Deposit selects (the filter, `CapitalMovementFormDialog` and the import row) stay native and show the name only, because they need a payload change that is not worth it for a pick list.

The change adds no token and touches no rule in `context/foundation/glossary.md` and no _Avoid_ term; the mark is a trait of appearance, not a Bank rule.

Supersedes in part ADR-0047: the paragraph "instead of the logo" (the short mark replaces the logo only where there is no logo) and the paragraph on `AccountIdentityBlock` (the logo stays on the Dashboard only); the mark table, the cash and `other` rules, the `aria-hidden` tile and the conditional Switch stay. Supersedes in part ADR-0045: the short text mark "instead of the logo" in the Accounts bank tile. Supersedes in part ADR-0051: the bank tile of Term Deposits as `AccountMarkCompact` "instead of `AccountIdentityBlock`". We rejected keeping the three SVGs (a coloured square with three letters is not a logo), a white tile with a new token (the logos fit on `muted`, and a new surface meaning is not needed), `object-cover` (it crops non-square logos), an Account column in the operation row (the row has no room, and the meta line carries the avatar), and the logo in `AccountBankSelect` (it picks a Bank and has no URLs).
