# Month Plan layout, brand for document state and approval, and the note Alert

The Month Plan follows the "C · Atrament i morski" system (artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/9gJSdtfpFgUAa8oEMgR4HA). `brand` means identity, navigation, document state and the one action that approves or saves the document. The Badge `brand` marks an approved plan ("Zatwierdzony"), as it already marks the "Realizacja" phase of a Budget, and the Button variant `brand` is used only for "Zatwierdź" and "Zapisz zmiany", at most one per view, never for the status of money. This supersedes in part ADR-0035: its "never status" means money status (over limit, paid, received), which keeps `success`, `destructive` and `warning`.

Alert gains the variant `note`: a `warning-soft` background, a `warning` icon, `foreground` text and no title. It carries one sentence about a reading shown on the same page, under the metrics panel or under the table (pace ahead of the elapsed days, drawing past available liquidity). The `warning` variant stays for other non-blocking warnings.

The page layout is: a `StatPanel` with To Spend, its two components (income and expenses by the To Spend rule in `context/foundation/glossary.md`, the rule itself unchanged) and Pace, without a pace bar; one table with the groups Income, Expenses and Put aside / take and a Status column (no "N% of plan" status, because it would be a new number in the logic pair); a put-aside row with a direction switch; an edit bar in `primary` stuck to the bottom of the screen, only in change mode, with "Zatwierdź" in the title row; a past approved month as a full read-only view with a "Końcowy" badge; and a state dot for every month in the month strip.

We rejected plan sums as the Income and Expenses measures (the equation in the To Spend caption would stop adding up), the edit bar at the top as in the mockup (saving falls out of reach on a long table), a separate Notice component (two warning components), an "N% of plan" status (a new number in the logic pair), a past month with only the summary (the category view is lost) and "Dodaj operację" in this view (a separate change).

Superseded in part by ADR-0060: the Month Plan table has a column "Zatwierdzony" before "Plan" with a direction arrow, and the caption of the approved amount ("Zatwierdzono: …") is gone.
