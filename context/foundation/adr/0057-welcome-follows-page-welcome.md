# The welcome page follows the PageWelcome card of the design system

The page before sign-in (`pages/Welcome.vue`) and its two components (`LandingAppPreview.vue`, `LandingFeatureCard.vue`) go back to the `PageWelcome` card of the "C · Atrament i morski" design system (artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/9gJSdtfpFgUAa8oEMgR4HA, `project/components/PageWelcome/preview.html` and the `.ht-land*` classes), as it stands on 2026-11-07. The card is marked "Propozycja" in the artifact; this ADR records where the repo follows it and where it departs from it, and from earlier ADRs. The change is `welcome-atrament-ui`.

Class names. As in ADR-0056 the `ht-*` names of the artifact are mapped, not copied:

| Artifact | Repo |
| --- | --- |
| `ht-land`, `__bar`, `__wrap` | `.landing-bar` (sticky bar), `.landing-wrap` (content width) |
| `__section` | `.landing-section` |
| `__hero`, `__title`, `__points` | `.landing-hero`, `.landing-hero-title`, `.landing-points` |
| `__grid`, `__feature`, `__icon` | `.landing-grid`, `.landing-feature`, `.landing-icon` |
| `__steps`, `__step-no` | `.landing-steps`, `.landing-step-no` |
| `__cta` | `.landing-cta` |
| `__foot` | `.landing-foot` |
| `__preview`, `__side`, `__nav-item`, `__main`, row | `.landing-preview`, `.landing-preview-side`, `.landing-preview-nav-item`, `.landing-preview-main`, `.landing-preview-row` |
| `display`, `heading`, `lead`, `card-title` | the named styles of the same names |

Dimension tokens. Three values join the dimension tokens in `:root` of `app.css`, with no `.dark` variant: `--landing-width` (1120 px, the content width of every section; `.landing-wrap`), `--landing-bar-height` (56 px, the bar and the `scroll-margin-top` of the anchors `#features` and `#how`, so a section heading does not land under the sticky bar) and `--control-height-lg` (36 px, the hero and call-to-action buttons). The 36 px height is the `@utility h-control-lg` next to `h-control-sm`, not a class in `@layer components` and not a size in `ui/button`: the height of the `default` size is the `@utility h-control`, and the comment in `app.css` says a class from `components` does not override a utility, so a `.landing-cta .button` rule would lose. `Button` merges its classes with `cn` (tailwind-merge), which may not treat `h-control` and `h-control-lg` as one conflict; the computed height (`getComputedStyle(el).height === '36px'`) is therefore checked in the browser. If 32 px wins, the fallback is `size="lg"` with the class `h-9` (tailwind-merge replaces `h-10`), recorded here as a departure. The buttons in the bar keep the `default` size (32 px), as in the card, which sets 36 px only in the hero and in the call-to-action band.

Text styles. `display` gains `letter-spacing: -0.025em` from the card. It has no other consumer (ADR-0056 left it for this change), so the spacing is written in the style itself. The `h1` of the hero is 30/36 below `sm` and `display` (36/40) from `sm`, as in the card; the `h2` of a section is `heading`, the lead is `lead`, the title of a feature is `card-title`, the label above a section heading is `label-caps`. `heading` and `lead` now have a second consumer (the panel of ADR-0056 and this page); `card-title` its first.

Panels. Every panel of the page is a `card` with a full `border` and `rounded-lg`, with no resting shadow and no half-transparent border (ADR-0037): the bar, the feature panels, the steps, the preview and the footer. This replaces `rounded-xl`/`rounded-2xl` and `border-border/60`. The bar is 56 px on `card` with a full border below, with no `bg-background/80` and no `backdrop-blur`. The four steps are one panel `.landing-steps` with four cells numbered "01"–"04": a column with a `border-t` below `md`, a row with a `border-l` from `md`. `.stat-panel` is not reused, because it holds measures with a label and a value, not steps. A feature panel is `.landing-feature` with a 32 px tile `.landing-icon` on `accent` (`radius-md`), not the shadcn `Card` and not a 40 px `rounded-xl` tile.

Call-to-action band. `.landing-cta` is a dark panel on the `--sidebar-*` tokens, as `.auth-aside` (ADR-0056): `sidebar-background` with `sidebar-foreground` text, the `h2` (18/24) in `sidebar-strong`, `radius-lg`, focus `sidebar-ring`, and the action is `Button variant="brand"` in `h-control-lg`. It replaces `bg-accent`, which is the colour of badges and tiles and so did not set the closing call apart. No new colour: the tokens are the same in both themes. The contrast of the two new pairs is measured in Phase 2 of the change and written below.

Contrast of the band (measured in Phase 2).

| Pair | Light | Dark | Threshold |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `brand-foreground` on `brand` (button text) | 8.56 : 1 | 8.56 : 1 | 4.5:1 |
| `brand` outline on `sidebar-background` (non-text; also the `sidebar-ring` focus outline) | 9.63 : 1 | 10.92 : 1 | 3:1 |

Already measured while researching: `sidebar-foreground` on `sidebar-background` 7.50:1 / 6.86:1, `sidebar-strong` on `sidebar-background` 14.69:1 / 16.48:1. Both new pairs pass in both themes, so no token changes. The values come from a one-off script (WCAG relative luminance, sRGB) over the hex values in `:root` and `.dark` of `app.css`; the script is not committed.

Application preview. `LandingAppPreview.vue` is built from the blocks of the Dashboard (ADR-0045) instead of a sketch of dots: `StatPanel` with three measures (Do wydania, Płynność, Łączne środki) with no `toneClass`, so no measure is in `text-success` (`success` is income, and the measures are not income); a panel "Wydatki" in `ListSurface flush` with a `table-header` row and four rows `.landing-preview-row`; in each row `CategoryChip`, amounts through `formatMoneyAmount` in the `amount` style, `ExecutionMeter` with `yearElapsedPercent` 84, and the footer "Kwoty w zł" (ADR-0037). The grid `.landing-preview-row` has four columns (`minmax(0,1fr) 74px 74px 104px`: category, plan, realisation, meter), as in the card, not the five of `.category-meter-row`: there is no "Pozostało" column, and the Dashboard class is not changed. Below `sm` the "Plan bieżący" column is dropped, so a 390 px screen has no horizontal scroll. The sidebar of the preview is a static replica of the current app sidebar (ADR-0055), built from the `--sidebar-*` tokens on plain `div`/`span` elements, with no `SidebarProvider` (`useSidebar()` needs one): `BrandMark` with the product name as in `AppLogo`, the `.sidebar-search` row "Szukaj i przejdź" with a `kbd`, the year segments (`.sidebar-year-segments`, 2026 selected), the "Menu" label and the views of `useAppViews` with icons and shortcut `kbd`s (Dashboard active on `sidebar-primary`), then a separator, `.sidebar-hint` and a user row with a neutral sample name and initials. The classes are `.landing-preview-side` (216 px, hidden below `sm`) and `.landing-preview-nav-item` (the geometry of a `SidebarMenuButton`); the preview grid is 216 px + content and the hero columns are 0.8fr/1.4fr so the table keeps its category names. Fixture data sits in a local constant typed `DashboardCategory`; `pages/dev/dashboard-fixtures.ts` is not imported into a production page. The data of the preview is a mock-up, not the user's.

Overrun in the preview. The Zdrowie row (113%) carries `Badge variant="destructive-soft"` "przekroczono" (`t.budget.over_limit_badge`) next to the figure. The card shows the overrun with the figure and a red bar only; ADR-0045 says colour alone does not carry a status, and the preview teaches the code of the product, so it follows ADR-0045, not the card. This is the one departure of the preview from the card.

Preview metrics. The measures of the preview use the utility `landing-kpi` (mono 15/24, weight 500) instead of `kpi` (22/28), and `.landing-preview .stat-panel-metric` sets `px-3 py-3`, so three measures fit the narrow preview panel; both are in `app.css` and affect only the landing.

Not focusable. The preview is `aria-hidden="true"` and holds nothing focusable: no `Link`, `a` or `button`; the sidebar items and the table rows are `div`/`span`. `CategoryMeterRow` is not used for this reason (its root is an Inertia `Link`); only its cell markup is copied.

Product mark. The sidebar of the preview shows `BrandMark` with the product name, as the real sidebar does (`AppLogo`); an earlier draft of this ADR showed the mark alone and is superseded. This narrows the sentence of ADR-0053 that `LandingAppPreview.vue` "keeps its decorative tile": the preview now shows the real mark, not a tile, and the rule "the mark is always with the name" has no exception. `design-system.md` is corrected: `LandingAppPreview.vue` reads the sidebar tokens, not `sidebarMenuButtonVariants`.

Registration link. "Zarejestruj się" stays behind `canRegister` in the bar and in the hero, with the literal `href="/register"`. Registration is off (`config/fortify.php` has no `Features::registration()`), so `canRegister` is `false` and Wayfinder generates no route for it. This is an exception to ADR-0056 ("links use Wayfinder routes, without a hand-written `href`"), limited to `Welcome.vue` and to that one address; the guard `ui-contract.test.ts` forbids `href="/` in the three files with the exception `/register`. When registration is enabled, a Wayfinder route replaces the literal and the exception disappears. The change does not enable registration and adds no route.

Breakpoints. The bar navigation appears from `md`, the hero splits into two columns from `lg` and the steps form a row from `md`, as the page works today and as the entry screens switch at `lg` (ADR-0056). The 860 px of the artifact is not added: it is a value that is neither `md` nor `lg`, and the repo has no custom breakpoints.

Texts. Five keys of `landing` in `lang/pl/ui.php` follow the card: `features_lead` loses "bez zbędnego marketingu", `feature_privacy_description` says "klucze dostępu" instead of "passkeys" and drops "w MVP", and `hero_lead`, `feature_liquidity_description` and `feature_month_description` replace the dash with a comma. The preview adds keys `preview_metric_to_spend`, `preview_metric_liquidity`, `preview_category_housing`, `preview_category_health` and `preview_year_progress` where `dashboard` and `budget` have none; the other labels (`nav.*`, `dashboard.total_funds`, `dashboard.expenses`, `budget.phase_execution`, `budget.over_limit_badge`, `budget.amounts_in_zl`) are existing keys. `preview_category_salary` goes if nothing reads it after the change.

Guards. `WelcomeHardcodedUiTest` (a pattern map as in `DashboardHardcodedUiTest`: palette classes, arbitrary values, Tailwind text sizes, `rounded-xl|2xl`, `border-border/N`, `max-w-6xl`, `size="lg"`, `shadow-sm`, `tracking-tight`, `backdrop-blur`, `text-success`), the three files in `scripts/check-ui-literals.mjs` and a block in `ui-contract.test.ts` (no `href="/` except `/register`, no `text-success`, no `<Link`, `<a ` or `<button` in the preview, `BrandMark` in the page and in the preview). Every state is shown in the kitchen sink `/dev/ui/welcome` (`local` only): `?state=guest|guest-register|authenticated` renders the real `Welcome` with the shared props `auth` and `canRegister` overridden (Inertia merges shared props shallowly, page props win), so `Welcome` gets no new prop and `defineProps` does not change.

We rejected the 860 px breakpoint, the prefix `ht-`, the `--space-*` tokens (the repo maps them to the Tailwind scale), a size or a variant in `ui/button` for 36 px, editing `ui/sidebar` or wrapping the preview in `SidebarProvider`, reusing `CategoryMeterRow` and `DashboardStatPanel` (an Inertia `Link` and a `Form` in an `aria-hidden` region), a "Pozostało" column and a change of `.category-meter-row`, importing the dev fixtures into a production page, `text-success` for the measures of the preview, the shadcn `Card` for features, `.stat-panel` for the steps, removing the registration link until registration exists, adding a route `register`, and an e2e test for the landing (the visual gate is the screenshots from the sink). It changes no rule in `context/foundation/glossary.md` and no _Avoid_ term, adds no Inertia prop and no package.
