# Dashboard layout follows Atrament

The Dashboard follows the "C · Atrament i morski" system (artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/9gJSdtfpFgUAa8oEMgR4HA), like the Budget (ADR-0037), Operations (ADR-0038, ADR-0039) and the Month Plan (ADR-0042). This supersedes in part `context/archive/2026-09-23-dashboard-hierarchy/spec.md`: user stories 15-17, 19-22, 25 (the execution part) and 28, and the details "Headline tiles", "Page grid", "Budget area", "Category sections" and "Holdings list". The current-year rule (ADR-0031), the notice kinds, severities, order and links (ADR-0032, ADR-0033) and the attention-zone grid with its limit of six cards stay.

The page order is: title row, First Run checklist, one metrics panel, the attention zone, then a grid with the budget execution panel on the left and the Accounts and Term Deposits panels on the right. This departs from the spec, which put the attention zone before the headline tiles, and records the order the page already had. The right column has a fixed width, the token `--aside-width` (300 px), instead of about 2/3 and 1/3. The token is added with the shared layer of this change.

The metrics panel is one `StatPanel`: To Spend of the current month (the first measure, with a bar of elapsed days from the `month_pace` prop, the month name in the label, the "propozycja" badge only for a proposal and the POST link to the month plan), Spendable Liquidity, Total funds and Year Elapsed. The number of measures follows the state: 4 for a Budget in execution with a month plan, 3 for a draft or without a month plan (no To Spend), 2 without a Budget. Plan sums (Current Plan with Planned Balance) show only for a draft; in execution the group rows of the execution panel carry the plan, and Planned Balance stays on the Budget page.

Budget execution is one panel with the tabs Wszystko / Dochody / Wydatki (`SegmentedControl` `neutral`, `segmented-control-tabs`). The tab is local state and every visit starts on "Wszystko", so the Dashboard opens in the same form (story 29). Above the Categories of each type stands a group row with the sum and an `ExecutionMeter`. The columns are Kategoria, Plan, Zrealizowano, Pozostało, Wykonanie and Status, and Status is the Badge "przekroczono" for an exceeded Category. All Categories show, with no limit of five and no expand, so the panel reads like the Budget list. A row links to Operations filtered to the Category and the current year, built only with `operationsListQuery`.

Year Elapsed is a measure in the metrics panel with the as-of date ("na dzień"), and the mark stands on every meter that may show it (income and Paced expense Categories, not One-off), always, with no checkbox and no `localStorage` key. `context/foundation/glossary.md` carries the rule.

A notice becomes a card in the style of `ht-notice`: a neutral border, `rounded-md`, the border colour changes on hover, severity only in the icon tile (`warning-soft` or `destructive-soft`) and in the kind label. The content stays as it is: the identity (Account, Term Deposit or Category) and the signed amount. The payload, the kinds, the order, the links and the six-card limit of ADR-0032 do not change.

Accounts and Term Deposits are two panels with the action "Wszystkie" in the header, rows (bank mark, name, balance) and a footer with the sum of the visible rows ("Łączna płynność" / "Suma lokat"). There is one markup at every width: no accordion on a phone and no remembered expand state.

We rejected the tab in the URL (the Dashboard always opens in one form, and the filter-keeping helper of Operations has no reason to reach here), a limit of five with an expand (a second way to see the rest, and a total that does not match the visible rows), a sentence with the numbers inside the notice card (new copy for seven kinds and a changed payload), footers from server props (an archived Account can keep a balance, and Spendable Liquidity sums it, so the figure would not equal the visible rows), the attention zone before the metrics panel (the page already shows the measures first) and "Dodaj operację" in this view (a separate change).

## Aktualizacja

The notice card has two lines instead of the identity and the right-hand amount: a title in `text-sm font-medium` and one muted `text-xs` sentence with the numbers, both left-aligned beside the icon tile centred on the text block (a `<button>` card needs `text-left`). Both are built in `DashboardNotices.vue` from the existing payload, with the copy in `lang/pl/ui.php`; the payload, kinds, order, links and the six-card limit do not change. This changes "The content stays as it is" above: the kind label, `AccountIdentityBlock`, `CategoryChip` and the right-hand amount leave the card, and the amount moves into the sentence (an absolute value for an overrun or shortfall, signed for a negative balance). It also reverses the rejected "sentence with the numbers inside the notice card": the sentence is composed in the component from fields the payload already carries, so no PHP or payload change is needed.

The same pass aligns the rest of the view with the mock: the percentages in the metrics panel and the execution footer are whole numbers, the day bar is made of dashes, the "Plan miesiąca →" link shares one line with the day caption, the execution meter has a fixed track with a small monospaced percentage right behind it, Accounts and Term Deposits rows carry a small mark, a name and a caption (bank, "Portfel" or "<bank> · do <date>") with a bold footer without the "Kwoty w zł" line, and the title line shows the full Polish date.

Superseded in part by ADR-0045: the metrics panel has three measures without Year Elapsed, budget execution is one panel with two tables (Dochody, Wydatki) and the Switch "Postęp roku" instead of tabs, group rows and the Plan and Status columns, and a notice card has the kind label, the identity and the value on the right. The Konta and Lokaty panels stay.

Superseded in part by ADR-0061: the "fixed width" of the right column. `--aside-width` is a `clamp()` and grows with the window.

Amended by ADR-0066: the Year Elapsed mark stands on income and on every expense Category except an Irregular one, instead of “Paced, not One-off”.
