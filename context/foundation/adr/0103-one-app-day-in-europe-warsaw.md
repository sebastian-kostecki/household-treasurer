# One App Day in Europe/Warsaw

The app reads „today” from three clocks. The server counts it in UTC (`CarbonImmutable::today('UTC')`, `before_or_equal:today`), the year menu, the command palette and the day labels read the local clock of the browser, and seven dialogs take their default date from `new Date().toISOString().slice(0, 10)`, which is the UTC day of the browser. The year-turn rehearsal (`year-turn-rehearsal`, step T2, gap 4) showed the split: at 00:30 Polish time on 1 January 2027 the year menu marks 2027 as „bieżący”, while the Dashboard and the default date of an operation dialog still show 31 December 2026. This ADR gives the app one day.

Rule. The App Day is the calendar date of the current instant in the Europe/Warsaw zone. Every „today”, „current month” and „current calendar year” in the domain is the App Day, its month or its year: the Dashboard year and the default year (ADR-0031, ADR-0075), a month past, current or to come in the Budget and the Month Plan, approving and changing a plan, Year Elapsed, Month Elapsed, Month Pace, Collected, Opening Collected and „prognoza”, the reports („w toku”, year over year, the last twelve months, the Trend, Years and its cut day), the start of a Rhythm and the year of archiving a Category, and every „date not later than today” rule in a hand entry, in an Import, in a balance reconciliation and in an operation rebuild.

Server. One source in `app/Support`: `App\Support\AppDay`, with `today(): CarbonImmutable` returning the Europe/Warsaw date at 00:00. Controllers and readers take the day from it and pass `$today` on as they do now; the calculations that accept `$today` do not change. `before_or_equal:today` compares with the App Day date and not with PHP `today` (Laravel resolves the text rule `today` with `Date::parse` in the application zone and the rule takes no zone, so S-39 replaces the 15 rules with the `Y-m-d` date of `AppDay`, such as `Rule::date()->beforeOrEqual(AppDay::today())`; a zone inside the text, `today Europe/Warsaw`, gives 23:00 UTC of the day before while the value is parsed at 00:00 UTC and refuses the App Day itself). `today('UTC')`, `today()` and `CarbonImmutable::today()` without a zone, `now()->year` and `now()->toDateString()` are not used for a domain decision. `config/app.timezone` stays `UTC`: timestamps (`created_at`, `approved_at`, `archived_at`, sessions, tokens, backup file names) are instants in UTC, not an App Day. The day of an instant: when a timestamp becomes a day or a month (a comparison with a month, as `archived_at` in the Proposal and in Collected, or a displayed date), its date in Europe/Warsaw counts, not its date in UTC. Example: a Category archived on 1 February 2027 at 00:30 in Poland (31 January 23:30 UTC) is archived in February.

Browser. A shared prop `shell.today` (`'YYYY-MM-DD'`) from the same source. The default dates of the dialogs (operation, own transfer, capital movement, term deposit, account, balance reconciliation, with its `max`), „bieżący” and `createYear` in the year menu, the command palette, the new Budget dialog, the „this month” and „previous month” presets, the year in the day labels and the guest pages (the year progress on the login screen, the year in the footer of the welcome page; `shell` reaches them too) take the day from `shell.today` and not from `new Date()`. The rule has no exception. A page left open over midnight keeps its day until the next full Inertia visit (a partial reload with `only` does not refresh `shell`); yesterday's date is still valid, and the server decides about a „future” date anyway.

Examples. At 31 December 2026 23:30 UTC (1 January 2027 00:30 in Poland) the App Day is 2027-01-01: the Dashboard says „1 stycznia 2027”, the year menu marks 2027 „bieżący”, the default date of a dialog is 2027-01-01, and an operation from a bank statement dated 01.01 is not „future”. At 31 December 2026 22:59:59 UTC (23:59:59 in Poland) the App Day is 2026-12-31 and December can still be approved. In summer (CEST) 30 June 22:30 UTC is already 1 July.

Time changes. Midnight in Europe/Warsaw always exists and is one (the changes happen at 02:00 and 03:00); the day has 23 or 25 hours, so code moves the calendar day and never by 24 hours.

Year turn. The year and the month change at 00:00 Polish time. The hard deadline for approving December is 31 December 23:59:59 Polish time. The README section „Przełom roku” and the tests pinned to UTC midnight change together with the code (S-39), not earlier.

Rehearsal clock. `app:rehearsal-clock` keeps setting the instant (input in UTC); the App Day follows from it, and the browser follows through `shell.today` without `page.clock`.

Data. No migration: business dates are `date` columns, and the stored start months of Rhythms are not recalculated. The month of archiving is not stored, it follows from `archived_at`; existing timestamps stay, and from S-39 their day is read in Europe/Warsaw (an archiving in the first hour (two in summer) after Polish midnight moves to the next day).

Relation to earlier ADRs. It supersedes in part ADR-0074, ADR-0075, ADR-0076, ADR-0077, ADR-0081 and ADR-0097 (the sentences „in UTC”) and ADR-0098 (the sentence about `new Date().getFullYear()`). ADR-0031 and ADR-0087 inherit the App Day without a change. ADR-0013 (the backup schedule in Europe/Warsaw) stands.

Consequences. S-39 (`one-app-day`) introduces `AppDay` and `shell.today` from the code inventory in the plan of the change `app-day-decision` (section „Spis miejsc w kodzie”, quoted without a path because the folder moves to `context/archive/`); it changes the props on both sides in one commit with an `assertInertia` test; it adds the rule against a clock in the domain to `.ai/rules`; S-40, S-41 and S-43 take the day from `AppDay`.

We rejected:

- UTC on the server with the browser matched to it (for an hour in winter and two in summer the whole app is in yesterday, and an operation dated today from the bank is refused as future);
- the whole app in Europe/Warsaw (`config/app.timezone`, `TZ`: stored `timestamp without time zone` values shift by 1–2 hours without a migration, and the hour of a time change is ambiguous);
- the browser counting Europe/Warsaw itself (two implementations of one rule, the rehearsal clock needs `page.clock`, and a wrong device clock gives another day than the server).
