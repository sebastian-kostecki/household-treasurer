# The session year follows the year turn

A User who chose 2026 in the year menu in December and keeps the session alive over Polish midnight still lands in 2026 in January, while the Dashboard is already in 2027 (year-turn rehearsal, gap 3, step T3). `SelectedBudgetYear` returns the session `budget_year` whenever the User has a Budget for it (ADR-0075), and the session does not record when the year was chosen, so „2026 is left over from December” cannot be told apart from „in January I deliberately went back to 2026”. Sessions last 120 minutes of inactivity, so the gap bites only a session kept alive across midnight with a year chosen before it. This ADR decides which session year gives way at the turn.

Rule. A choice of a Budget year in the session (the year menu, a new Budget) records the App Day year in which it was made, in the session key `budget_year_chosen_in`, next to `budget_year`. A session year S chosen in App Day year C is not a choice once the App Day year is later than C and S equals C: `S === C && AppDay::today()->year > C`. The default year of ADR-0075 then applies. Every other session year stands: the order of ADR-0075 still starts with the year in the session when the User has a Budget for it. The switch is silent: no toast, no notice. The reading stays free of side effects (it is called more than once per request), so the stale session year is ignored and not forgotten. A session year without a recorded choice year, or with a malformed one, stands (sessions from before this ADR expire within 120 minutes of the deploy). Deleting the Budget of the session year forgets both keys.

Examples. 2026 chosen on 31.12 at 23:40 Polish time (22:40 UTC) is 2026 at 22:59:59 UTC and 2027 from 23:00:00 UTC, which is 1.01.2027 00:00 in Poland (the default year is 2027 when it has a Budget, whatever its phase). 2023 chosen in December 2026 for browsing stays in January 2027. 2027 chosen, or created as a new Budget, in December 2026 stays in January. 2026 chosen again on 1 January 2027 stays: it was chosen in 2027, so S differs from C. 2026 chosen at 31.12.2026 23:30 UTC (App Day 1 January 2027, as after the re-login at rehearsal step T2) is a choice in 2027 and stays; to see the switch in a rehearsal the year is chosen before 23:00 UTC. A session year with no recorded choice year stands.

Relation to earlier ADRs. ADR-0075: the order of the default year does not change; the year in the session wins only while it is a choice under this ADR. ADR-0103: both „the year it was chosen in” and „the year now” are App Day years, so the switch happens at 00:00 Polish time. ADR-0031: the Dashboard always reads the current calendar year and is not affected. The rule covers every view that reads `SelectedBudgetYear`: Budget, Month Plan, Reports, the command palette and `shell.budgetYear`.

Consequences. `SelectedBudgetYear` gains `choose` and `forgetIfChosen`, the only writers of the session year, and `resolve` applies the rule. The test that pinned the gap (`test_session_year_of_the_old_year_stays_selected_in_january_known_gap`) is rewritten to the required behaviour as a change of requirement, not assertion tuning. The glossary entry Budget and the README section „Przełom roku” change with the code. No migration, no Inertia prop change.

We rejected:

- every choice made before the turn expires (rule B: it throws the User out of a year browsed at midnight, such as 2023, or of the next year prepared in December);
- a rule without a marker, such as „a session year earlier than the current one is ignored” (it forbids going back to 2026 in January);
- a one-time toast about the switch (a side effect in a reading called several times per request, plus new UI copy);
- a shorter session or clearing the sessions at the turn (it logs the User out and does not fix a session kept alive).
