# The entry screens follow the Page cards of the design system

The seven entry screens (`pages/auth/Login`, `Register`, `ForgotPassword`, `ResetPassword`, `VerifyEmail`, `ConfirmPassword`, `TwoFactorChallenge`), their layout `AuthCardLayout` and `PasskeyVerify` go back to the `PageLogin`, `PageRegister`, `PageForgotPassword`, `PageResetPassword`, `PageVerifyEmail`, `PageConfirmPassword` and `PageTwoFactorChallenge` cards of the "C · Atrament i morski" design system (artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/9gJSdtfpFgUAa8oEMgR4HA, `project/components/Page*` and the `.ht-auth*` classes), as they stand on 2026-11-07. The cards are marked "Propozycja" in the artifact; this ADR records where the repo follows them and where it departs from them, and from earlier ADRs. The change is `auth-atrament-ui`.

Layout. One layout serves all seven cards: `.auth-shell`, a grid with the dark panel `.auth-aside` (the `--sidebar-*` tokens, the same colours as the sidebar, so no new colour) next to the card `.auth-card`. The widths are the dimension tokens `--auth-aside-width` (440 px) and `--auth-card-width` (400 px) in `app.css`, with no `.dark` variant. The layout switches at `lg` (1024 px), not at the 860 px of the artifact: 440 px of panel, 400 px of card and their margins do not fit side by side at `md` (768 px). Below `lg` the panel collapses to a bar with the product mark, which is `BrandMark` with the name (ADR-0053); from `lg` it shows the pitch, the year block, three points and the footer. The card holds an optional 36 px icon on `accent` (`.auth-card-icon`), the `h1` of the card, a `meta` description, the body and an optional footer (`.auth-card-foot`). The card is a `card` with a full `border` and `rounded-lg`, as every panel (ADR-0037), not the shadcn `Card` with `rounded-xl` used until now.

Class names. The repo names classes without a prefix (`.form-panel`, `.sidebar-year-segments`, `.command-palette`), so the `ht-*` names of the artifact are mapped, not copied:

Confirmation colour. A confirmation that carries no amount ("link sent", "new link sent") is `<Alert variant="ok">`: `accent` background, `accent-foreground` text, icon and title, transparent border. Until now it was bare `text-success`, which a reader takes for income. This is an exception to two rules of `design-system.md`: `accent` is "not for status" and `success` is "only amounts". The precedent is Badge `ok`, which is already `bg-accent text-accent-foreground` (`ui/badge/index.ts:23`), so `accent` already means "ok" for a status; the Alert extends that meaning, it does not add a new one. The exception is limited to a confirmation with no amount: an amount keeps `success` and `destructive`, and an error stays `destructive` (`AlertError`, `InputError`). The variant sits in `ui/alert`, like `note` (ADR-0042) and `danger` (ADR-0049), because it has three consumers (Login, ForgotPassword, VerifyEmail), so it is not an edit for one screen. The contrast of `accent-foreground` on `accent` is checked in both themes (4.5:1, ADR-0035); a failure is fixed by a token change recorded in a new ADR.

Primary action. The 34 px block button is the named class `.auth-submit` (`w-full`, `h-control-field`, centred) in `app.css`, with no new size in `ui/button`: 34 px only makes sense next to the 34 px fields of the card (ADR-0040), and `ui/button` is not edited for one screen. The "resend" action of VerifyEmail is `variant="outline"` with the same class, as in the card.

One-time code. `.auth-otp` sets the size of the slots of `InputOTP` (40 × 44 px, JetBrains Mono 18/500, 8 px gap, a dash after the third character) without editing `ui/input-otp`. If the base classes of `InputOTPSlot` win over the override, the rule becomes a descendant selector `.auth-otp [data-slot=input-otp-slot]` in `@layer components`.

Text styles. `app.css` gets four named styles from the artifact: `display` (36/40, 600), `heading` (24/30, 600), `lead` (15/22) and `card-title` (14/20, 600), next to `page-title`. The `h1` of an entry card is `page-title` (18/24), as in the artifact; `heading` and `lead` are read by the panel; `display` and `card-title` have no consumer until the change that moves `Welcome` to them (one view per change). They are listed in `.ai/rules/css.md` and in the typography table of `design-system.md`.

ConfirmPassword. The card `PageConfirmPassword` shows one password field and "Potwierdź". The repo keeps the passkey button above it, with the separator `.auth-or` ("lub"), because the function exists in the code (`ConfirmPassword.vue`) and removing it would remove a way to confirm. This is the only departure from that card; its state is in the kitchen sink.

Tab order. The order of the keyboard is the order of the DOM, with no `tabindex` on any entry screen: passkey, e-mail, "Nie pamiętasz hasła?", password, "Pokaż hasło", remember me, sign in. The link "Nie pamiętasz hasła?" stands in the label row of the password field, before the field, as in the markup of the card (the README of the card describes another order; the markup wins). `PasswordInput` loses `tabindex="-1"` on its toggle, so "Pokaż hasło" is reachable by keyboard also in the settings (`Security`, `DeleteUser`), which share the component.

Year block. The panel shows "Rok budżetu", the progress of the year and twelve month tiles (past, current, future), as in the card. A guest has no budget, so the block is not read from the server: the pure function `authYearProgress(today)` in `resources/js/lib/auth-year-progress.ts` computes it from the date on the client, in local time, with no Inertia prop. The tiles are `aria-hidden`; the figures and labels come from `t.auth.aside.*`.

Reset without an e-mail. `GET /reset-password/{token}` without `?email=` passes `email = null` (`FortifyServiceProvider`). The field is `readonly` on `muted` only when the prop holds an address; without it the field is editable (`autocomplete="email"`) and the submitted value is the field, not the prop. The prop type becomes `string | null` on both sides in one commit with an `assertInertia` check.

Texts and routes. Every text of the entry screens comes from `t.auth.*` (`lang/pl/ui.php`), with no English literal, and links use Wayfinder routes; the registration link exists only behind `canRegister` and without a hand-written `href`. `TextLink` becomes the one link style (`brand-text`, `font-medium`, `hover:underline`), with `as="button"` for the switch of the two-factor mode.

Guards. The entry files join the list of `scripts/check-ui-literals.mjs`, `ui-contract.test.ts` fails on `tabindex`, `text-success`, `href="/` and `'/dashboard'` in them, and every state is shown in the kitchen sink `/dev/ui/auth` (`local` only), where `?screen=<screen>&state=<state>` renders the real page component with fixture props, so the layout, `setLayoutProps` and `Form` behave as in production.

Card footer. The footer of the card (`.auth-card-foot`) is filled through `AuthCardFooter`, a `Teleport defer` to `#auth-card-foot`, because Inertia pages render into the layout's default slot and cannot fill a named slot. The app has no Inertia SSR entry or server (`config/inertia.php` enables SSR, but there is no `ssr.ts`, no `inertia:start-ssr` and no `bootstrap/ssr`), so the teleport and the year block (`new Date()` in the layout) assume client rendering; if SSR is introduced, both need a second look. The sink has no `error` or `recovery` state: the Inertia `Form` fills `errors` only after a rejected submit, so a fixture prop would not show; those screens are captured from a real submit.

We rejected the 860 px breakpoint of the artifact (it does not fit next to 440 + 400 px), the prefix `ht-` (the repo has none), a `block` size or a variant in `ui/button` and a size override in `ui/input-otp` (both are for one screen), the colour `success` for the confirmation (it means income), a year block read from the server (a guest has no budget) and removing the passkey from ConfirmPassword. It changes no rule in `context/foundation/glossary.md` and no _Avoid_ term. It adds one Inertia prop type change (`ResetPassword.email` may be `null`), on both sides in the same commit with an `assertInertia` check, and no package.
