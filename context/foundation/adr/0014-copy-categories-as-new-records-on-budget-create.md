# Copy Categories as new records on Budget create

Creating a Budget may copy another of the User’s earlier Budgets by inserting new Category rows (name, type, icon, color, sort order, Current Plan) onto the new draft. We rejected a year-spanning Category identity or template: FR-CAT-02 stands, Financial Operations stay on the source Budget, and same names across years are a coincidence of copy. We also rejected a separate “copy into this Budget” verb and a category checklist in the add-year dialog — the pain is starting a year, and the draft already allows delete and add.
