# Report Trend: the year spread over twelve months, in whole złoty

S-30 (FR-017) adds a third view to Reports, „Trend”, that spreads a Budget year over its 12 months. ADR-0070 fixed what a report row is and ADR-0074 added the year before; this ADR fixes what the Trend shows, which cells are empty, how amounts are rounded and how a table of twelve month columns fits the screen, before the code. It adds to ADR-0070 and changes none of its clauses.

Source. The Trend is the year report without the last summing step: the same read of `MonthCategoryActuals` for the 12 months, the Budget Lines with their list Category and the User’s Category Groups, in `ReportTrend` (`app/Support`). Rows, their order and the archived mark are those of the year report without a comparison (ADR-0070), taken from one shared step, `ReportCategoryTree`, so the two views cannot drift. The Trend does not call `BudgetMonthResult` (it reads one month per query); a test proves that the measures equal it. The amount of a row for the year equals its amount in the year report, and the sum of its month cells equals it, to the grosz. The number of queries is constant and not higher than the year report’s.

Cells. There are always 12 month columns, also for months to come. A Category cell is `null` („—”) when the Category has no operation in the month and `0` when it has some and the net is 0 (a purchase and its refund in one month); a lone refund is a negative cell. A group cell is `null` when none of its rows has an operation in the month, otherwise the sum of its rows, archived ones included. The measures of a month: income `null` without an income operation, expense `null` without an expense operation, result `null` only when the month has no operation at all; in the other cases a missing side counts as 0. A Category with no operation in the year has no row, a group has a row when it has at least one such row. The month in progress is the month of today (UTC, like ADR-0074) when the Budget year is the current year, otherwise none; the page marks it „w toku” and does not grey it or cut its amounts at today.

Whole złoty. ADR-0058 says every amount in a table has two decimals; twelve month columns plus a total do not fit a laptop with them. In the Trend, and only there, the table shows whole złoty with the footer „Kwoty w zł, zaokrąglone do pełnych złotych”. Each cell rounds its own exact amount half away from zero with integers only (149 gives 1, 150 gives 2, −150 gives −2); „Razem” rounds the exact sum, not the sum of rounded cells, so a total may differ by a złoty from the sum of the cells it shows; an amount closer to zero than 50 groszy is „0”, never „−0”. The server sends minor units and the client rounds for display; exact amounts stay in the Year and Month views. This is the one exception to „always two places”, limited to the Trend and to its chart axis.

Layout. The Trend table is the first table in the app that scrolls sideways. The columns share the width with `fr` weights and minimums (ADR-0061); at a 1280 px window with the sidebar open it fits without scrolling, and a narrower card scrolls horizontally inside the card, never the page. The name column is pinned to the left with an opaque background equal to its row, so scrolled amounts do not show through it, and the scrolled area can be reached with the keyboard. Only a table with a column for each month may use this; any other table keeps the rule of ADR-0061. Because 14 columns have no meaning without their headers when a screen reader reads them, the table carries table roles.

Chart. The month chart is a vertical grouped bar chart, two bars per month (income, expense), on the library of ADR-0071, which describes horizontal bars for the Period Report; the vertical form is the form of the Trend chart. Colours stay `--success` for income and `--chart-1` for expense, so no token is added. The chart is `aria-hidden`; the table of the measures next to it holds the same numbers.

We rejected:

- the Trend as a section under the year report, and a two-level switch (Rok / Miesiąc with a nested choice): it made the year page longer and hid a view behind a second control; a third segment is one click;
- cents in the table with horizontal scrolling on a laptop, and a mixed format (whole złoty in the cells, cents in the totals): the first hides the year behind scrolling, the second shows two roundings of one amount side by side;
- one chart per type and a chart of the selected row: the table already carries the rows, and a chart that changes with a selected row hides the year behind a click;
- greying the month in progress or cutting its amounts at today (the Trend shows what was booked, as the tiles and the Budget do; a mark is enough);
- a „Średnio / mies.” column (the divisor has no single meaning: months with operations, elapsed months or twelve);
- the year before inside the Trend (ADR-0074 keeps the comparison in the Year and Month views; two lines per cell would not fit);
- a link from a month header to the report of that month (declined by the User);
- colour or arrows for change from month to month (only the result row has a tone).
