# Opening Collected is a column of the Rhythm entry; edge cases of the Collected chain and the reach of the warning are fixed for S-26

ADR-0066 defines the Irregular Rhythm, the Irregular Rate and the Collected reading, and ADR-0072 the write rule of the Rhythm history, but both leave open where Opening Collected is stored, what the Irregular Rate is in the months the Month Plan does not cover (a draft Budget, a year without a Budget, an approved month without the item), how a Category created as Irregular reads the months before its first entry, what the „zebrane” line in a plan row means in a past, the current and a future month, and which months the warning on an operation and in Import checks. S-26 (US-02, FR-010, FR-011, FR-013, FR-022) needs these answered before the code. This ADR adds to ADR-0066 and ADR-0072; it changes none of their rules.

Storage. Opening Collected is the nullable column `category_rhythms.opening_collected_minor_units` (`bigint`, no default) of the Rhythm entry. Every Irregular entry starts a period: Collected at the start of its start month is the Opening Collected of that entry, and the period ends at the next entry. An entry of another Rhythm has `null`. The write rule: a new Irregular entry stores the typed Opening Collected (no amount means 0); marking the Rhythm the Category already has adds no entry and, when the amount differs, corrects it on the entry in force in the current month, which is the entry that started the current period; removing „nieregularna” and marking again within one month replaces that month’s entry (the delete-from-the-current-month rule of ADR-0072); the same Rhythm with a later entry (possible only after a restore) starts a period in the current month with the amount sent. `ChangeCategoryRhythm` stays the only way to write an entry. The column is added by its own migration that changes no row; its `down()` turns every Irregular entry into Even Annual and drops the column, which is lossy (the Opening Collected and the Irregular Rhythm are gone) and has a test.

F9 (a), a month the Budget does not run. A Financial Operation needs a Budget in execution (ADR-0067), so a month of a draft Budget and a year without a Budget cannot have a bill, and their Irregular Rate is 0: Collected stands through them. The phase is read when Collected is read, so starting the Budget later gives its unapproved months their rate. This is the same case as the draft Budget of 2027 in the example of ADR-0066.

F9 (b), an approved month without the item. An approved Month Plan that has no item for the Category gives the Irregular Rate 0, until the user adds the Category in „Zmień plan”. The formula does not step in, because an approved plan is the final statement of that month. Example: „Serwis auta” with Opening Collected 500 from March, March approved without the item, a bill of 700 in March → rate 0, surplus 200, Collected at the end of March 0.

F9 (c), months before the first entry. ADR-0066 reads a month before the first entry as the Rhythm of the first entry, so a Category created as Irregular reads as Irregular in October when its first entry starts in November. Those months have Collected 0 and belong to no period: their bills do not enter the chain. Example: „Serwis auta” created in November with Opening Collected 0 and a bill of 300 dated in October → the October row reads Irregular with Collected 0, and Collected on 1 November is 0, not 0 − 300 + rate.

The rate of a month in the chain, in this order: (1) a year without a Budget or a Budget not in execution → 0; (2) no Budget Line of the Category in that Budget → 0; (3) a month with an approved plan → the current amount of the item, 0 without an item (F9 (b)); (4) otherwise floor(present Current Plan / 12), December the remainder (the one formula `App\Support\AnnualPlanShare`, shared with the Proposal), 0 when the Budget Line was archived before the first day of the month. The archive state and the phase are read when Collected is read.

The „zebrane X” line of a plan row. X is Collected at the end of the viewed month. In a past month this is the state at its end; in the current month it is the state as of today (the bills dated so far); in a future month it is a forecast with the bills that already exist. „Stawka” is the amount in the column „Plan”; the word stands in the hint of the window in „Kategorie”, not in the table. The row shows one number, not „before → after the bill”.

The reach of the warning. The warning on an operation (hand entry) and in Import checks the month of the operation date and every later month with a plan on which the entry acts through Collected, because a bill lowers Collected and with it the starting point of the later months. Rows of one Import are chained: a later row sees the Collected the earlier rows left. Nothing is blocked. Example: Collected on 1 November is 600 and the rate 150, and a bill of 700 is already entered in November. An overdue bill of 400 dated in September, entered now, makes Collected on 1 November 200, so the surplus of November becomes 700 − (200 + 150) = 350 and To Spend of November falls by 200. The warning for the September bill names November, not only September.

31 December. The chain passes through 31 December already in S-26: Collected on 1 January is Collected at the end of December, and the rate of January comes from the plan of the new year (ADR-0066). S-27 only adds showing „zebrane startowe” on the screen of a new Budget (FR-012).

Snapshot. Version 10 carries Opening Collected on each entry of the `rhythms` list (a number ≥ 0 for Irregular, `null` for the others); the month of marking is the entry’s start month. Version 9 still restores, without the amount, and refuses an Irregular entry, which that version never wrote (S-26, phase 2).

We rejected:

- storing Opening Collected on the Category or on the Budget Line (a Category has several periods over time, and the amount belongs to the entry that started one);
- a separate table of periods (the entry already holds the start month and the Rhythm);
- the formula for an approved month without the item (the approved plan is the final statement of the month, and the user adds the item in „Zmień plan”);
- reading the months before the first entry as the current one, or letting their bills enter the chain (one read rule of the Rhythm for every Rhythm, ADR-0072);
- a „before → after the bill” line, and hiding „zebrane” in future months (one number, also as a forecast);
- a warning only for the month of the operation date (a bill moves Collected of the later months with a plan);
- computing Collected only on the server (the plan preview and the warnings react before the save, so the arithmetic has a TypeScript copy, `.ai/rules/month-calculators.md`);
- a `down()` that throws (a lossy restore with a documented rule is better than none, ADR-0072).
