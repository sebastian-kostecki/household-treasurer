# „Lata” opens in Full Years while the current year has no closed month

S-43 (`years-report-in-early-january`, gap 5 of the year-turn rehearsal) fixes what „Lata” shows on the first days of January. ADR-0097 makes the Same-day Cut („Do D”) the default and ADR-0101 gives the Category Panel a tab „Lata” that counts the current year up to the same day. On 1 January the cut is 1 January: every earlier year is counted for one day, so „Zmiana od 2020” and „Średnio rocznie” compare one day of 2027 with one day of 2020, and the same holds on 2 January for two days. The comparison says nothing about the years. This ADR records the rule before the code, as AGENTS.md requires for a change to a glossary rule. It changes these clauses and no others: ADR-0097:9 („Do D” is the default) and :25 (the panel tab „Lata” „by default with the Same-day Cut”), and ADR-0101:11 (the panel counts up to the same day in the current year, so that the bars equal the tiles). ADR-0101's „no mode switch in the panel” and ADR-0074 stand unchanged.

Rule. The default range of „Lata” depends on the App Day (ADR-0103). When the cut day D falls in January, the current year has no closed month and the default is Full Years („Pełne lata”); in any other month the default is the Same-day Cut („Do D”) as in ADR-0097. The rule holds for the whole of January, 1–31, not for a fixed number of days. One exception: when the current year is the only column (the first year with a Budget in execution), January keeps the Same-day Cut as the default, because Full Years would count no column at all (the only year is „w toku”, no final year, no change) and there is no earlier year whose one day could mislead; the current year is not cut, so „Do D” shows that year whole. The table keeps its switch „Do <D> stycznia | Pełne lata” in January, and „Do D” counts exactly as before. With a draft current year (January with a draft Budget for the new year) nothing changes: there is no current year in execution, every year is full and the switch is not shown (ADR-0097). The server decides the default, because the client reads no clock; it sends the default range with the Years data and the page only starts from it.

Category Panel. The tab „Lata” of the Category Panel follows the same rule: in the current Year it counts the Same-day Cut except in January with an earlier column, where it counts Full Years (caption „Pełne lata”, the current year „w toku”); in every other Year it counts Full Years as in ADR-0101. The panel still has no mode switch. In January the bar of N−1 is the whole year N−1, while the N−1 tile of the panel stays the year-over-year amount up to the same day (ADR-0074). This amends the sentence of the reports rule that the bars of the viewed year and of the year before equal the tiles, for January only; from February they equal the tiles as before.

Year over year. The Year and Month reports do not change. On 1 January they compare with 1 January of the year before (ADR-0074: a period in progress is cut at today minus one year), with `is_partial: true` and the existing note. S-43 adds tests of that comparison (the January month, the amounts, the page at Polish midnight) and no code.

Example (illustrative numbers). On 2 January 2027 with Budgets 2020–2027 in execution, Full Years shows the columns 2020–2026 whole and 2027 „w toku”. Razem 2020 = 42 000 and 2026 = 74 000 give „Zmiana 2020–2026” +76,2% ((74 000 − 42 000) ÷ 42 000) and „Średnio rocznie” +9,9% ((74 000 ÷ 42 000)^(1/6) − 1, 6 years). The Same-day Cut on that day would compare 2 days of 2020 with 2 days of 2026. One click on „Do 2 stycznia” shows that table.

Boundary. The App Day changes at Polish midnight (ADR-0103). In winter that is 23:00:00 UTC: at 22:59:59 UTC on 31 December the App Day is 31 December and the default is the Same-day Cut; at 23:00:00 UTC it is 1 January of the new year and the default is Full Years. At 22:59:59 UTC on 31 January the App Day is 31 January and the default is still Full Years; at 23:00:00 UTC it is 1 February and the default is the Same-day Cut.

We rejected:

- a cut to the end of the last closed month (in January there is none, and in every other month „Do D” would count something other than the table's name says);
- a hint or banner in „Lata” (the table would stay misleading until the User read it, and it adds UI copy);
- a fixed first 7 or 14 days of January (a rule with no reason in the data: the current year has no closed month for the whole of January);
- a mode switch in the Category Panel (ADR-0101 keeps the panel without one);
- a whole previous year for the Year and Month reports on 1 January (ADR-0074 rejects it for a period in progress, and a month compared with a whole year is wrong).
