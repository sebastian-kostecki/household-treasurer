# One Category list across years; the yearly plan lives on a Budget Line

A Category belonged to one Budget, and the years were joined by a query over name and type in five places (the January Proposal of ADR-0033, the Operations filter of ADR-0016, the filter chip, the two-stage Import suggestion of ADR-0034, the Dashboard and command palette links). Copying Categories onto each new Budget (ADR-0014) made every year a separate set to edit, rename and archive. PRD v3 (FR-001, FR-006) asks for one list. This ADR supersedes ADR-0014 entirely and changes clauses of ADR-0016, ADR-0018, ADR-0030, ADR-0033 and ADR-0034 as set out below. Rhythm (the four rhythms, where they live, “collected”, 31 December) is F-05 and not decided here; merging Categories (FR-023) is S-21; the safe-migration rules and the database copy before it are F-04.

Model. A Category belongs to the User, once for all years, and has a name, icon, colour, sort order, type (Income or Expense), an optional Category Group and a description (FR-002). A Budget Line is one Category in one Budget and holds that year’s Initial Plan, Current Plan and Execution. Financial Operation, Cash Expectation and Month Plan item point at a Category, in the Budget of the year of their date or month. Until F-05 changes it, the Paced / One-off flag and the Month Item Kind stay on the Budget Line unchanged, so no month’s To Spend moves.

Joining Budget Lines. A new Budget gets Budget Lines from the active Categories of the list, with the plan suggested from the previous year’s Current Plan (FR-005). A Category created or restored on the list joins every draft Budget, and always joins with plan 0. It joins a Budget in execution, and a draft where it is missing (after “Usuń” or after the migration), at its first use in that year, again with plan 0: a Financial Operation (by hand or from Import), a Cash Expectation, an amount in a Month Plan, or a plan typed in the Budget. Edge case: „Domeny” created on 10 December plus an 80 zł operation dated 2026-12-10 give a 2026 Budget Line with plan 0 and Execution 80, and a Budget Line in the 2027 draft with plan 0; Budget 2025 is untouched.

Names. The name key is the name trimmed and case-insensitive (Unicode); diacritics count, so „Prad” and „Prąd” are two Categories. The pair (name key, type) is unique across the whole list of the User, archived Categories included. Creating a Category or renaming one to the name of an archived Category is rejected with a hint to restore it. An income „Zwrot” and an expense „Zwrot” may coexist.

Global archive. An archived Category disappears from the choice in operations, Month Plan and a new Budget (PRD v3, structural decision 4, FR-003, FR-019). It stays in the history, in the Budgets and in the reports of years where it has Execution; on an existing operation it appears in the selector only while already selected. Restoring returns it to the choice and adds its Budget Lines to the drafts (as above). Archive has two levels. The archive of the list Category (global) only decides choice: operations, Month Plan, new Budget. The Budget Line has its own `archived_at`; Budget totals, Dashboard counters, the archive section of the Budget and the Month Plan Proposal read the state of the Budget Line, not of the list. Archiving a list Category removes its Budget Lines from drafts and sets `archived_at` on its Budget Line in the Budget in execution of the current calendar year; Budget Lines of other Budgets do not change. Restoring clears `archived_at` of that Budget Line and adds Budget Lines to the drafts. Example: „Wakacje” (archived 2026-09-09) keeps its 2026 Budget Line with that date and stays in the archive section of Budget 2026 as today. Archiving „Rekolekcje” in March 2027 sets `archived_at` on its 2027 Budget Line, so it drops out of the 2027 totals, while Budget 2026 does not change.

Type and deletion. “Usuń” in a draft Budget removes only the Budget Line of that Budget; the Category stays on the list. A list Category can be deleted only when it has no Financial Operation in any year, no Cash Expectation and no Budget Line in a Budget in execution; deleting it also removes its Budget Lines in drafts. Otherwise there is archive or merge (S-21). Its type cannot change under the same predicate (a Financial Operation in any year, FR-002; a Cash Expectation, as ADR-0018 says today; a Budget Line in a Budget in execution, as `UpdateCategoryRequest` says today). A type change to a taken key (name key, new type) is rejected, and a type change takes the Category off its Group.

Category Group. A group is an attribute of the list Category, so moving a Category applies to all years and all reports (a 2025 report does not reproduce the old groups). A group has a type and accepts only Categories of that type, has no plan and no settings, and its name is unique within the type (same normalisation as above). Deleting a group leaves its Categories without a group; a group has no archive. After the migration there are no groups.

Migration and snapshot (FR-006, FR-022). Steps, in this order, with no amount changed:

- (a) the name key of every old Category record is computed as above;
- (b) in every Budget, records with the same key (name key, type) are numbered ordered by ascending id. Record no. 1 joins the list Category of that name; record no. n ≥ 2 joins the Category „<name> (k)”, where k is the (n−1)-th free number ≥ 2. A number is taken when the key „<name> (k)” belongs to any original record of the User of the same type in any Budget, so record no. n gets the same k in every Budget. The name before the suffix is shortened so that the whole fits in 255 characters;
- (c) name spelling, icon, colour and sort order come from the newest Budget (highest year, draft included) that has the Category;
- (d) a Category is active only if it has a non-archived Budget Line in the newest Budget of the User (highest year, draft included) or in the newest Budget in execution; otherwise it is archived, with an archive date equal to the latest `archived_at` of its Budget Lines, or the migration moment when none is archived. The migration adds no Budget Line to drafts: an active Category without a Budget Line in a draft joins it at first use;
- (e) every old Category becomes a Budget Line with the same amounts, flags and `archived_at`, and operations and Month Plan items point at the same list Category in the same Budget;
- (f) groups are empty and descriptions are empty.

A snapshot of version 1 to 5 goes through the same steps on restore (snapshot version 5 holds Categories as a flat list with `budget_id`). Dev example: 2026 #23 and 2027 #70 become „Rozrywka”, 2026 #46 becomes „Rozrywka (2)” and is active, the same for „Odsetki” and „Odsetki (2)”; „Wakacje” becomes archived; Pensja, Mieszkanie and the remaining #40–#47 are active without a Budget Line in the 2027 draft; a Category present only in 2025 is archived. The plan and Execution of every 2026 Budget Line stay as they were.

ADR-0014 is superseded entirely: a new Budget takes the active Categories of the list with the plan from the previous year’s Current Plan (FR-005), and there is no copying of records. New wording of clauses:

- ADR-0016: the Operations filter is by list Category (id) across all years, and a rename no longer splits the history.
- ADR-0033: January reads December of the same list Category in the previous year’s Budget (an id, not a lookup by name and type); a Month Plan item is a Category with a Budget Line on that Budget; Month Item Kind goes onto a new Budget from the Budget Line of that Category in the previous year, with defaults when it has none. The rest of the Proposal rule stands.
- ADR-0034: votes are counted per list Category, not per name-and-type pair. The target is the winning Category if it is active and the year of the row’s date has a Budget in execution; without such a Budget there is no suggestion, and the Budget Line joins at commit. The threshold, k = 5 and ties do not change. The sentence that there is no Category identity across years no longer holds.
- ADR-0018: “date year equals the Category’s Budget year” becomes: the year of the date must have a Budget (draft or execution), and the Category joins it as a Budget Line; the block on deleting and on changing the type stays.
- ADR-0030: an Earmark is keyed by the list Category, applying it defaults to the same Category and the User may pick another; Align after Year Close concerns every expense Budget Line of that Budget.

We rejected:

- every Category in every Budget (old years would show Categories that did not exist then);
- blocking an operation on a Category without a Budget Line (an extra step, also in Import);
- merging duplicates in the migration (it changes the amounts of a Budget Line) and halting the migration (manual work against FR-006);
- exact comparison of names and a list without uniqueness (ambiguous Import and year comparison);
- an archive only after an explicit archive in the last year (old Categories would return to selectors);
- a group per year (comparing years would confuse moves with spending);
- no deleting at all, and deleting with Budget Lines in a Budget in execution;
- year-spanning identity as a “lineage” column on per-Budget records (still two Categories to edit);
- moving the flags from the newest Budget onto the list in S-18 (it would change To Spend of the current month).

Amended by ADR-0066: the Rhythm is an attribute of the list Category with a history, not a flag on the Budget Line; the Paced / One-off flag and the Month Item Kind stay on the Budget Line only until S-24 moves their readers, and the January Proposal clause on Month Item Kind is void.
