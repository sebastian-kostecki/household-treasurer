# Reports redesign: the Years view, the Ranking with a Category Panel, and chart colours

F-07 (`report-redesign-decisions`, milestone M-4) records the decisions of S-34 (the Years view), S-35 (Ranking and Category Panel) and S-36 (panel tab „Lata”) before their code, as AGENTS.md requires for a change to a glossary rule and to a term marked _Avoid_. ADR-0070 fixed the report row and its order, ADR-0071 the chart library, ADR-0074 the year before, ADR-0077 the Trend, ADR-0081 the last twelve months and ADR-0084 the states of the view. This ADR adds a fifth period, „Lata”, puts a Ranking by amount in place of the table in list order and of the bar chart in the Year, the Month and the last twelve months, adds a Category Panel to the Year, and fixes the colours of the chart marks. It changes these clauses and no others: ADR-0070:11 („The report never sorts by amount”) and ADR-0070:19 (the rejection of „sorting rows by amount”); ADR-0071:13 (the async chart component), :15 (colours), :17 (a chart is `aria-hidden`, the table is its accessible form), :19 (horizontal bars, one per first-level row), :23 (new series with `--chart-2..5`) and :29 (the rejection of „CSS bars”); ADR-0074:7 (a finished period is compared whole; the cut to „today’s day minus one year” stays for N − 1, and „Lata” adds its own cut), :15 (the series „rok wcześniej” has a chart to sit in) and :23 (no percent change); ADR-0077:9 (whole złoty „only there”) and :11 (sideways scrolling only in the Trend); ADR-0079:5 (the order of groups and Categories „as in the reports”); ADR-0081:17 (the switch changes the year report) and ADR-0084:5 (four segments) and :7 (room for the bar chart).

Superseded in part by ADR-0103: the current calendar year and the cut day D are taken from the App Day (the date in Europe/Warsaw), not from UTC („the current calendar year (UTC)” and „The cut day D is today’s day in UTC”); the rest stands.

Years. „Lata” is the fifth period of Reports, next to the Year, the Month, the last twelve months and the Trend. It is a table of Categories by year: one row per Category, one column per Budget year in execution, from the earliest to the current calendar year (UTC). Drafts and years to come are left out (today the columns are 2020–2026, and a draft 2027 has none). A year without a Budget in execution inside the range has no column. The range does not depend on the selected Budget year: changing the year in the sidebar does not change the table and leaves the User in „Lata”, unlike ADR-0081:17 for the twelve months, because the table has no „selected year”. When the current year has no Budget in execution (January 2027 with a draft 2027), the table ends with the last year in execution, every year is full, and the switch „Do …” is not shown. With no Budget in execution there is an empty state „Brak budżetu w realizacji”, as in the twelve months. The view is resolved before the phase of the selected Budget, so a selected draft does not take it over (as ADR-0084 for the twelve months). Polish copy: the segment „Lata”, the title „Raport: lata 2020–2026”, the switch „Do 14 listopada | Pełne lata”, tabs „Wydatki | Przychody”. Named terms: Same-day Cut („do tego samego dnia”), Full Years („Pełne lata”), Change since First Year („Zmiana od 2020”, „od 2022”), Average Yearly Growth („Średnio rocznie”) and Fastest Growing („Najszybciej rośnie”).

Same-day Cut. The cut day D is today’s day in UTC. In the mode „Do D” (the default) a year Y counts the operations from 1 January Y to the day D of the year Y inclusive, without overflow: 29 February gives 28 February in a year that is not a leap year. The current year is not cut (as ADR-0074:7, „The current column is not cut”): it holds also operations dated after today and equals the year report; only the earlier years are cut at D. Example: on 14.11.2026 the year 2020 is counted from 01.01.2020 to 14.11.2020. Leap example: on 29.02.2028 the year 2027 is counted to 28.02.2027, the year 2024 to 29.02.2024, and the label is „Do 29 lutego”. The switch of the range is not the „compare” toggle of ADR-0074:25, which stays rejected: it chooses what a column counts, not whether a second series is shown.

Full Years. In the mode „Pełne lata” every finished year is counted whole, and the year in progress shows its amount as the year report does (no cut, ADR-0074:7) with the note „w toku”, but it does not enter the change, „Średnio rocznie”, „Najszybciej rośnie” or the bold highest year; the change header is „Zmiana 2020–2025”. Example (illustrative full years): Razem 2020 = 42 000, 2025 = 74 000, 2026 (the whole year, no operation dated after 14.11) = 68 240 gives „Zmiana 2020–2025” +76,2% ((74 000 − 42 000) ÷ 42 000) and „Średnio rocznie” +12,0% ((74 000 ÷ 42 000)^(1/5) − 1, 5 years). In the mode „Do 14 listopada” from the card: 35 980 → 68 240 is +89,7% and „Średnio rocznie” +11,3% (6 years). With no finished year in the range (the only year in execution is the current one) the change and the statistics show „—”.

Columns. Groups as in the year report, rows in the order of the Category list (ADR-0069), and the group row holds the sums, the change and the sparkline. Every Category of the list with at least one Financial Operation in any counted range of years has a row, also an archived one (marked „zarchiwizowana” and muted); „Razem” stands once per type. Amounts are Execution as in the Period Report: refunds net, without Own Transfer and Capital Movement. In the mode „Pełne lata” the amount of a finished year equals the amount of that Category in the year report to the grosz, and „Razem” of the year equals „Wydatki” or „Przychody” of the year report; in the mode „Do D” the current year equals the year report in progress. Tabs „Wydatki | Przychody”. The highest year of a row (among the years counted for the change) is bold. In S-34 the Category name is plain text; S-36 makes it a link to the report of the current year (the last year in execution when the current year has no Budget in execution) with the panel of that Category open on its tab „Lata”, and the click changes the period segment to the Year. S-35 names the URL parameter of the panel and of the view tab together with the parameter of the selected Category.

Cells. „—” means the Category has no Budget Line in that year; 0 means it has a line and the net is 0 or there is no operation in the range. A group cell is „—” when none of its Categories has a line in that year. The sparkline of a row has the same numbers as the row, scaled to the maximum of the row; a year „—” is a short line in `track`, a negative amount is a zero bar (the table stays exact), and the sparkline is `aria-hidden`.

Change. Change since First Year is (amount of the final year − amount of the first year with an amount in the row) ÷ amount of the first year, in tenths of a percent rounded away from zero, computed from minor units. The final year is the last column in the mode „Do D” and the last full year in „Pełne lata”. When the first year of the row is not the first column the note says „od 2022”. The cell is „—” when the row has no amount in the final year (an archived Category such as Kino), when the amount of the first year is not positive, or when the first year is the final year. The tone is that of ADR-0074:13: a rise of an expense is `destructive`, a fall is `success`, and the reverse for income. Example from the card: Meble „+19,4% od 2022”, Internet −8,3% in `success`. „The first year with an amount” is the first column with an amount above 0; a cell 0 and a cell „—” do not count, and the same definition holds for the threshold of four columns below.

Statistics. Average Yearly Growth of the „Razem” row is (end ÷ start)^(1 ÷ (final year − first year)) − 1 in tenths of a percent, where start is „Razem” of the first year with an amount above 0 (the definition of Change) and „—” when there is no such year, when it is the final year, or when the final amount is negative; example 35 980 → 68 240 over 6 years is +11,3%. Fastest Growing is the Category (not a group) with the highest Average Yearly Growth counted from its first year with an amount to the final year, among the Categories with an amount in at least 4 columns and in the final year; a tie goes to the earlier one in the list, and with no candidate the tile shows „—”. Example from the card: Wakacje „+25,5% rocznie od 2020”. The change, Average Yearly Growth and Fastest Growing are computed for the active tab („Wydatki” or „Przychody”).

Amounts. The table and the sparkline of „Lata” use whole złoty like the Trend: each cell rounds its own amount away from zero, „Razem” rounds the exact sum, the footer says „Kwoty w zł, zaokrąglone do pełnych złotych · „—” = kategoria bez pozycji w budżecie tego roku”. Percents come from minor units. The panel of statistics shows złoty with grosze. On a narrow screen the table scrolls sideways with the Category column pinned. The exception of ADR-0077:9 (whole złoty „only there”) and of ADR-0077:11 (the first sideways table) now covers the Trend and „Lata”; every other table keeps two decimals and the rule of ADR-0061.

Ranking. In the Year, the Month and the last twelve months the table of groups and the bar chart are replaced by a Ranking: rows sorted by the amount of the period, descending, a tie goes to the order of the list (Czynsz 1 800 and Prąd 1 800: the one earlier in the list of Categories stands higher), and the rows that exist only through the comparison (no operation in the period) at the end in the order of the list. The switch „Kategorie | Grupy” defaults to „Kategorie”: Categories means all Categories flat with a position number; Grupy means groups and Categories without a group as first-level rows ordered by their sum, a group expanding to its Categories, also by amount. The share bar is the width of the row’s amount ÷ the largest first-level amount, and an amount that is not positive is an empty `track`. The column „Udział” is as in ADR-0070 (expenses only). „Różnica” is shown only with a comparison (the Year and the Month with N − 1 in execution) and stays an amount (ADR-0074:11); the twelve months have none. Archived rows are as today. Income stands under the expenses, its bar is `success` and it has no „Udział”. The Ranking sorts the result of the Period Report; `ReportCategoryTree::ofType` keeps the order of the list, because the Trend, „Lata” and the Operations filter read it (ADR-0093:9). Because the table has no bar chart beside it, `ReportBarChart` and its reserved room from ADR-0084:7 (`reportChartHeightRem(barCount, compared)`, the `Skeleton`) go away, and the series „rok wcześniej” (`--chart-2`, ADR-0074:15) has no recipient in the app; the token stays reserved for that role. In the Year the name of a Category opens the Category Panel; in the Month and the twelve months it links to Operations as today (S-31). The Trend and „Lata” stay in the order of the list. The order of the list stays the order of the Budget and of „Kategorie” (ADR-0069); the Ranking is the one report reading that is not.

Category Panel. The panel exists in the Year only. The selected Category is in the URL (S-35 names the parameter) and has `aria-selected`; with no choice the first position of the expense Ranking is selected, with no expenses the first of the income Ranking, with neither there is no panel. Content: the chip and „Wydatek · grupa Codzienne · rytm bieżąca”; the amount of the year, the amount of N − 1 (with a comparison), the difference as an amount and „±x,x% względem N−1” (the percent only in this caption, „—” when the amount of N − 1 is not positive), „Udział” with „największa pozycja” at position 1; a section „Wydatki w czasie” or „Przychody w czasie” with the switch „Miesiące 2026 | Lata” (S-36 builds the tab „Lata” from the same Years read, by default with the Same-day Cut); 12 month bars in `chart-1`, the highest month in `foreground`, the month in progress hatched in `chart-1`, months to come empty; „Średnio na pełny miesiąc (sty–paź)” is the sum of the closed months ÷ their number (a finished year: 12), „Najwięcej: <month>” (a tie goes to the earlier month), „<Month> do <D.MM> (w toku)” only in the current year; the button „Operacje: <kategoria>, <rok>” leads to Operations with the range of the year (S-31). „Średnio na pełny miesiąc” with no closed month (January of the current year) and „Najwięcej” with all months not positive show „—”. Amounts are in złoty with grosze. A percent is allowed only in „Lata” (Change since First Year, Average Yearly Growth, Fastest Growing) and in the caption of the difference in the panel; the column „Różnica” of the Ranking stays an amount, so ADR-0074:23 holds for it. The panel is not designed for the Month and the twelve months, and „Lata” for the Month and the twelve months is parked.

Navigation. The period switch has five items: „Rok | Miesiąc | 12 miesięcy | Trend | Lata”. „Lata” is the last, the Year stays first and the default, and its address is `?view=years`. ADR-0084:5 lists them with the fifth segment, and every state of ADR-0084 shows the title row with five segments. The Years view is resolved like `?view=last-12` (ADR-0081:17), before the phase of the selected Budget.

Colour. The colours stay the tokens of the app; the values come from `resources/css/app.css`. The fill of an expense (the share bar of the Ranking, the sparkline of „Lata”, the month bars of the panel, the bars of the Trend) is `chart-1`; the fill of an income is `success`; the current year in the sparkline and the highest month in the panel are `foreground`; a year without a line in the Budget is a line of 2 px in `track`; the month in progress in the panel is hatched in `chart-1`. `chart-2` keeps the role „the same period one year earlier”. `chart-3..5` stay unused. The cards of the Atrament design system paint fills through `chart-1`, and `chart-1` and `chart-2` are added to its `tokens.json`. The share bar, the sparkline and the 12 month bars are CSS or SVG marks from tokens, without Unovis and without `defineAsyncComponent`: this changes ADR-0071:13 and :29, since the rejection of „CSS bars” concerns charts with an axis and a scale, and Unovis stays for the chart of the Trend. The bar and the sparkline repeat the numbers of their row, so they are `aria-hidden`; the month bars of the panel have no table beside them, so they carry an accessible `sr-only` list of 12 amounts („<miesiąc>: <kwota>”, months to come left out) and the bars are `aria-hidden`. This changes ADR-0071:17: the accessible form of a mark is the table or this list. A new colour meaning is entered in the section „Wykresy” of `context/foundation/design-system.md`.

Rules to change with the code. S-34 and S-35 rewrite these sentences of `.ai/rules/reports.md` together with their code: the chart beside the table in the same order, `ReportBarChartPlaceholder` and the reserved room for the bar chart, „four segments” of the switch, the „only here” of whole złoty (the Trend and „Lata”), and the rule that a chart is `aria-hidden` with the table as its only accessible form. This ADR does not edit the rule file.

We rejected:

- `brand-text` as the fill of the marks: one token would mean a link and a chart, and the Trend and its tests would change for no gain;
- a separate glossary entry „Multi-year Report”, „Years Comparison”, „wieloletni raport” or „historia kategorii” (the Years view is one paragraph of the entry Period Report, next to „Last twelve months” and „Trend”);
- the Ranking only in the Year (the Month and the twelve months would keep a table and a bar chart that the Year no longer has), and the panel in every period (the Month and the twelve months have no design for it);
- the change to the year in progress in „Pełne lata” (a partial year would look like a collapse against finished years);
- a range of years up to the selected year (changing the year in the sidebar would silently change the table);
- the switch „Do D | Pełne lata” as a compare toggle (it was rejected in ADR-0074:25 for a different thing: a second series);
- a percent column „Różnica” in the Ranking (ADR-0074:23 stays for it);
- a „Bez grupy” block in the Ranking by groups (ADR-0069).
