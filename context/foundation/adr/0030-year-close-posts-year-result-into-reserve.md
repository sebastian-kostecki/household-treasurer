# Year Close posts Year Result into Reserve; leftover limit is an Earmark

Year Close posts that Budget’s Year Result into a User-owned plan-plane Reserve (unlabeled remainder plus Earmarks). We rejected a second pot of leftover Available Limit stacked on Year Result (that double-counts underspend) and rejected treating Reserve as cash or as a Category. The User picks Earmarks from positive leftover on any expense Category (Paced or One-off), totalling at most max(0, Year Result); we rejected limiting candidates to One-off. A loss year creates no Earmarks; negative leftover is ignored because overspend already sits in Year Result. After posting, Align runs on every expense Category of that Budget. A later Year Close on the same Budget replaces that Budget’s previous post. Applying an Earmark or unlabeled remainder into a later year is opt-in Current Plan on a later Budget only (not the year just closed), defaulting to the same Category name and type with a User override. Partial apply and release of an Earmark back to unlabeled remainder are allowed. A later loss reduces unlabeled remainder first (that remainder may go negative) and does not silently consume Earmarks. Liquidity does not move.
Amended by ADR-0063: an Earmark is keyed by the list Category, not by name and type; applying it defaults to the same Category with a User override, and Align concerns every expense Budget Line of that Budget.
