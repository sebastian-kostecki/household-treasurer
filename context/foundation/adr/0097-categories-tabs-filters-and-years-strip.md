# Categories in tabs with a search, a Rhythm filter and a years strip

Since the sheet load (ADR-0096) the list has Categories of the years 2020 to 2027, and the „Kategorie” page of ADR-0067 and ADR-0069 stopped being readable: two long panels one under the other, a collapsed „Archiwum (n)” section, the Rhythm in the caption under the name and the years as text („2020 · 2021 · …”). S-37 (MS-04, M-4) takes the layout of the `PageCategories` card (variant B) of the Atrament design system. ADR-0067 and ADR-0069 do not say how the list is split, searched or filtered, and nothing in them forbids it. This ADR settles the layout. It supplements ADR-0067 and ADR-0069 and changes none of their rules; the order of the list, the groups, the archive and delete conditions and the order save stay as they are.

Tabs. The page shows one panel of the active tab: „Wydatki” | „Przychody” | „Archiwum”, each with a count, as a `SegmentedControl` in the `neutral` tone. The tab is in the address as `?tab=income` or `?tab=archive`; „Wydatki” has no parameter, so an old `/categories` link opens it. The server validates the value and returns it as the `tab` prop, as `?section=` does for Operations (ADR-0038), and a change of the tab replaces the history entry, so „Operacje” from the „⋯” menu and Back return to the same tab. The archive is a tab instead of a section under the list, with a sub-section of each type („Wydatki”, „Przychody”) in the order of the list; a sub-section without a match is not shown, and the archive stays flat, without groups. „Dodaj grupę” is not offered in the archive; „Dodaj kategorię” there opens the window with the type „wydatek”.

Search. One phrase is shared by the tabs. It is matched against the name and the description of a Category, ignoring case and Polish diacritics („prad” finds „Prąd”), not against the name of a group or the label of a Rhythm. The tab counts show the Categories that match, so the user sees in which tab „Zwrot” is; without a filter they are the full numbers. The „n aktywnych” badge in the title always counts all active Categories. The phrase is kept in the state of the page, not in the address or in `localStorage`.

Rhythm. The Rhythm of the current month (ADR-0072, ADR-0066) is a column „Rytm” with a `Badge` and a short label (Stała, Bieżąca, Roczna, Nieregularna), only for expenses, and it leaves the caption under the name; the caption is the description, or „Zarchiwizowana DD.MM.RRRR · opis” in the archive. It is also a single-choice filter („Wszystkie rytmy” and the four Rhythms, with counts after the phrase) in „Wydatki” and in the archived expenses of „Archiwum”. Incomes have neither the column nor the filter. The filter is kept in the state of the page.

Groups under a filter. With a phrase or a Rhythm, a group stays with the Categories that match and says „k z n kategorii”; a group without a match disappears, and without a filter an empty group stays. The order is saved only from the full tree of the type (ADR-0069: the server rejects an incomplete set with 422), so under a filter the handles, the dragging and „W górę” / „W dół” of a group are not available, and the view never saves the filtered tree. „Zmień grupę…” stays, because it has its own endpoint for one Category. The confirmation of deleting a group states the full count of the group, not the visible one.

Years strip. „Lata w budżecie” is a strip `CategoryYearsStrip`: one cell per year of the axis of the user’s Budgets (`shell.budgetYears`, the draft included), a year with a Budget Line on `accent`, a year without one outlined with `border`. The accessible label is the ranges („Lata w budżecie: 2020–2022 · 2025”). A legend of the two cells stands next to the tabs. A year outside the axis is added to it, not lost.

Empty results and footer. A filter with no match says „Brak kategorii dla „:query”.” or „Brak kategorii o wybranym rytmie.” with the button „Wyczyść filtry”, which clears both. The footer of the panel says „n kategorii w m grupach” and the hint about the handle, or „k z n kategorii” without the hint under a filter, and only the number in the archive.

Rejected:
- a flat list of results instead of the groups (the context of the group would be lost);
- groups always visible under a filter (empty groups are noise, and a list with only „0 z n” groups hides the match);
- searching only in the active tab (the user would not know where „Zwrot” is);
- the archive as one list, newest first or in the order of the list (the columns would differ between the tabs, and the type would need a new marker);
- the Rhythm in the caption next to the column (it duplicates the column and cannot be scanned);
- the whole state, the phrase and the Rhythm too, in the address (every keystroke would be a history entry, or the entries would be replaced and Back would lose the tab);
- the state only in the page, the tab too (Back from „Operacje” would return to „Wydatki”);
- saving the order from a partial list (the server rejects it, and a computed order from a part of the tree would move Categories that are not on the screen).
