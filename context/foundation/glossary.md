# Household Treasurer

A single-user household budget and liquidity tracker: annual category plans versus real cash on bank and cash accounts. UI is Polish; this glossary is the English domain language used in code and agent docs.

## Language

**User**:
The person who signs in. Owns an isolated data space. Not shared with household members in MVP.
_Avoid_: Account (for the login identity), Customer, Member

**Account**:
A liquid holding the User owns: a bank account or a cash account. Has an opening date, opening balance, and current balance. Independent of any Budget year. A bank Account may have a Bank. An Account named by a Cash Expectation cannot be deleted; archive is allowed (existing Cash Expectations keep pointing; new ones cannot use the archived Account). Opening date cannot be later than the earliest Cash Expectation, Financial Operation, Own Transfer, or Capital Movement on that Account.
_Avoid_: Wallet, Portfel, BankAccount (unless disambiguating in a narrow type name), “account” for the User, Term Deposit

**Term Deposit**:
A holding of the User’s money in a bank term-deposit product. Not an Account and not a Category. Each Term Deposit is its own holding, not a pooled balance. Not an Own Transfer endpoint and not the Account of a Financial Operation. Has an opening date (the first day this holding is in the ledger, not the bank product start) and a single opening balance: that snapshot is not Income, not a capital movement, not a split of capital versus interest, and does not change any Account. Opening balance may be zero or negative. Current balance is that snapshot plus Capital Movements; interest does not change the Term Deposit. Current balance may be negative. Create or edit of that snapshot that would leave current balance negative is Negative Balance Warning, not a block. In-ledger capital in or out of the holding moves a liquid Account through a Capital Movement. Polish UI copy: „lokata”.
_Avoid_: Deposit (unqualified), Savings, Investment, Lokata in code, Account (for this holding)

**Bank**:
The named institution of a bank Account or Term Deposit: Alior, BNP Paribas, ING, Lokaty Facto, mBank, Pekao, PKO BP, Revolut, Santander, or Other. Required on bank Accounts and Term Deposits, absent on cash Accounts. Distinct from Import format (file layout of an export); Other here is not Import format Other. A named Bank does not imply a matching Import format.
_Avoid_: Import format, Connector, Integration, BankAccount (as this field’s name)

**Budget**:
The User’s plan and execution for one calendar year. At most one Budget per User per year. Without a choice in the session the app opens the current calendar year when it has a Budget, else the newest year not later than it, else the oldest (ADR-0075). Lives in draft, then execution. Year Close does not end execution: later Financial Operations dated in that year remain allowed. A new Budget gets a Budget Line for every active Category of the list, with the plan from the newest earlier Budget (a draft counts), and plan 0 when there is none (ADR-0068). The Rhythm is not copied: it is the history of the Category (ADR-0066).
_Avoid_: Year, Period, Wallet

**Initial Plan**:
The planned income amounts and expense limits frozen when execution starts, held per Budget Line (ADR-0063). Immutable afterwards, except that a Category Merge adds the Initial Plans of two folded Budget Lines (ADR-0078).
_Avoid_: Original budget, Baseline (in UI copy)

**Current Plan**:
The live planned income amounts and expense limits. May change during execution without touching the Initial Plan. It is held per Budget Line. Align sets it to max(0, Execution) on that Budget Line (Available Limit becomes 0). That rewrite is live Current Plan for every month of the Budget year; it is not Year Close and not a month-scoped close of Month Remaining. Year Close Aligns every expense Budget Line of that Budget after it posts. The User may later opt in to add an Earmark or unlabeled Reserve into a later Budget’s expense Current Plan; that is a Current Plan edit, not a second movement type.
_Avoid_: Revised budget, Forecast

**Budget Line**:
One Category in one Budget (ADR-0063). Holds that year's Initial Plan, Current Plan and Execution. The Rhythm is on the Category, not on the Budget Line (ADR-0066). A Financial Operation and a Month Plan item point at a Budget Line (ADR-0065). A Category created or restored on the list joins every draft Budget, and always joins with plan 0. It joins a Budget in execution, and a draft where it is missing (after „Usuń” or after the migration), at its first use in that year, again with plan 0 (joins with plan 0): a Financial Operation (by hand or from Import), a Cash Expectation, an amount in a Month Plan, or a plan typed in the Budget. „Usuń” in a draft Budget removes only the Budget Line; the Category stays on the list. Has its own archive state: Budget totals, Dashboard counters, the archive section of the Budget and the Month Plan Proposal read the state of the Budget Line, not of the list. A Category Merge re-points the Budget Line of the source to the target, or folds it into the target’s Budget Line of the same Budget (ADR-0078). Archiving a Category sets it on the Budget Line in the Budget in execution of the current calendar year and removes the Budget Lines in drafts; Budget Lines of other Budgets do not change. A Category of a Budget elsewhere in this glossary means a Category with a Budget Line on that Budget.
_Avoid_: Budget Category, Category (for this per-year record), Budget item, Month Plan item

**Category**:
An income or expense bucket that belongs to the User, one for all years (ADR-0063). Has a name, icon, colour, sort order (its place in the one order of its type, shared with Category Groups, ADR-0069), type, an optional Category Group and a description. The plan and Execution of a year are on its Budget Line: income Categories have a planned amount, expense Categories a limit. The name key is the name trimmed and case-insensitive (Unicode); diacritics count, so „Prad” and „Prąd” are two Categories. The pair (name key, type) is unique across the whole list of the User, archived Categories included; creating a Category or renaming one to the name of an archived Category is rejected with a hint to restore it. An income „Zwrot” and an expense „Zwrot” may coexist. A Category can be deleted only when it has no Financial Operation in any year, no Cash Expectation (on Agenda) and no Budget Line in a Budget in execution; otherwise there is archive or Category Merge (ADR-0078). Its type cannot change under the same predicate; a type change to a taken key (name key, new type) is rejected, and a type change takes the Category off its Category Group. An expense Category has a Rhythm (ADR-0066), changed only on „Kategorie” (ADR-0072). An item of a Month Plan is a Category with a Budget Line on that Budget. Archive is global with restore: an archived Category disappears from the choice in operations, Month Plan and a new Budget, and stays in the history, in the Budgets and in the reports of years where it has Execution; on an existing operation it appears in the selector only while already selected. Saving an existing operation with an unchanged archived Category passes when the Budget of the year of its date has a Budget Line of that Category, and an archived Category joins no Budget (ADR-0067). The selector offers an active Category in every Budget in execution, with or without a Budget Line (it joins at the save with plan 0); a Month Plan amount above 0 for a Category without a Budget Line creates it, an amount of 0 creates nothing (ADR-0067). The description is plain text, optional, up to 500 characters, shown only on „Kategorie” (ADR-0067). Restoring returns it to the choice and adds Budget Lines to the drafts. Dashboard meters and Dashboard plan totals (income, expense, Planned Balance) omit archived Budget Lines. Budget lists them in an archived section and omits them from plan totals unless the User asks to show archived. Month Remaining arithmetic still uses all expense Budget Lines of that Budget in the pool (Fixed Charge and Running), including archived. Migration: old Categories with the same name key and type in different Budgets became one Category; within one Budget they are numbered ordered by ascending id and the later ones get a suffix „(n)”; a Category is active only if it has a non-archived Budget Line in the newest Budget (highest year, draft included) or in the newest Budget in execution (ADR-0063).
_Avoid_: Tag, Label, Envelope, Category template, Category kind, Budget Line (for this list item), a per-Budget Category

**Category Group**:
An optional named group of Categories of one type, across all years (ADR-0063). Has no plan and no settings; sums the plan and Execution of its Categories in Month Plan, Budget and reports. Its name is unique within the type (same name key as Category). Its type is fixed when it is created. A group stands in the one order of its type (ADR-0069), shared with the Categories without a group; the Categories of a group follow it. Deleting a group leaves its Categories without a group, in the same places. In the Period Report a group sums all its Categories that have Execution in the period, archived ones included (ADR-0070); the Budget row of a group keeps only active lines (ADR-0069). In the Month Plan a group row sums Plan and Actual of its active Categories that are not idle Even Annual, shows „ponad plan” when any of them is over plan, and starts expanded (ADR-0079). Polish UI copy: „grupa”.
_Avoid_: Category (for the group), parent Category, subcategory, Envelope, a plan on a group

**Category Merge**:
Folding one Category (the source) into another of the same type (the target), both of the same User; the source disappears and its Budget Lines, Financial Operations, Month Plan items and Collected pass to the target (ADR-0078). The target must be active, the source may be archived. A Budget Line of the source in a Budget where the target has none is re-pointed to the target; where both have one they fold into the target’s (Initial Plan, Current Plan, Execution and Month Plan amounts add up). The Rhythm history of the target stays. Before the confirmation the window shows a preview computed on the server; the action saves a snapshot of the User first, and the way back is `user:restore` from it. The sums of every Budget and Month Plan are the same before and after. Polish UI copy: „scalanie kategorii”, „Scal z inną…”, „Scal”.
_Avoid_: join (that is a Category joining a Budget), link, combine, deduplicate, undo, „merge” for the list migration or for joining Rhythm entries

**Rhythm**:
An attribute of an expense Category, held as a history of entries (Rhythm, start month); the Rhythm of a month is the entry with the latest start month not after that month, and a month before the first entry has the Rhythm of the first entry (ADR-0066). Four values; each says what the item counts in To Spend and what a Proposal writes (table in ADR-0066):
- Fixed Charge („stała opłata”): plan, then the actual amount after the first operation with a positive amount; the Proposal writes the last paid amount.
- Running („bieżąca”): the greater of plan and actual; the Proposal copies the previous month; the only pool of Month Pace.
- Even Annual („równomierna roczna”): the greater of plan and actual; the Proposal writes floor(Current Plan / 12), the remainder in December; shows „zostało X na Y mies.”, X the Available Limit and Y the months to the end of the year including the current one; over plan when Execution of the year exceeds Current Plan.
- Irregular („nieregularna”): Irregular Rate plus surplus; the Proposal writes the Irregular Rate, as Even Annual; has a Collected reading.
A change works from the current calendar month; past months do not change. Every Irregular entry starts a Collected period and carries the Opening Collected as a column of the entry; the other entries carry none (ADR-0073). A Category Merge keeps the history of the target and drops that of the source (ADR-0078). A new expense Category is Running. An income Category has no Rhythm. The S-24 migration made the history from the flags the Budget Line had then: Paced + Running → Running, One-off + Running → Even Annual, Bill (either flag) → Fixed Charge; no Category became Irregular (ADR-0066, ADR-0072). Polish UI copy: „rytm” and the four names above.
_Avoid_: Paced, One-off, Month Item Kind, Bill (for Fixed Charge), frequency, schedule, cadence

**Paced Category**:
Historical: a flag on the Budget Line, replaced by Rhythm (ADR-0066) and removed in S-24 (ADR-0072). The migration mapped Paced + Running to Running and One-off + Running to Even Annual. Former Polish UI copy: „równomierna”; the pool was „pula równomiernych”; One-off „jednorazowa”.
_Avoid_: Envelope, monthly Category, scheduled Category, Available Limit (for the month figure), Running (the annual flag is not Month Item Kind)

**One-off Category**:
Historical: a flag on the Budget Line, replaced by Rhythm (ADR-0066) and removed in S-24 (ADR-0072); the mapping of the migration is under Paced Category. Former Polish UI copy: „jednorazowa”.
_Avoid_: Envelope, unbudgeted, unplanned, a Category with a due month, Bill (the annual flag is not Month Item Kind)

**Execution**:
Booked income and expense amounts against a Budget Line in a Budget that has started execution. Not unpaid Cash Expectations.
_Avoid_: Actuals (in code names; UI may say “wykonanie”), Cash Expectation, Agenda (as this plane)

**Available Limit**:
Current Plan of an expense Category minus booked expenses. Not cash on Accounts. Unpaid Cash Expectations do not reduce it. Not Month Remaining. A positive leftover on any expense Category is a candidate Earmark of a slice of Year Result, not a second pot. A negative leftover is not an Earmark: that overspend already sits inside Year Result.
_Avoid_: Balance, Available funds, Remaining cash, using this figure as Reserve or as an Earmark, Month Remaining

**Month Remaining**:
A signed reading, in Minor units, of leftover Current Plan for one calendar month of a Budget year: one expense Category, or the pool (all expense Categories of that Budget with Rhythm Fixed Charge or Running in that month). Not stored. Even Annual and Irregular have none. Arithmetic is catch-up leftover, not an even split of Current Plan. It is read only for past months without a Month Plan; the current and future months (and past months with an Approved Month Plan) show To Spend instead (ADR-0033). Polish UI copy: „Pozostało w miesiącu”.
_Avoid_: Available Limit, monthly limit, Envelope remaining, Spendable Liquidity, Year Elapsed, To Spend

**Year Elapsed**:
The fraction of calendar days of a Budget year that have passed as of an as-of date: day-of-year divided by days in that year. The as-of date is today when the selected Budget is the current calendar year, 31 December of that Budget year when it is past, and 1 January of that Budget year when it is future. A mark on a Category meter, not a money reading. Income Categories and expense Categories other than Irregular may show it; an Irregular Category does not. Distinct from Execution as a percent of Current Plan and from Month Remaining. Dashboard always reads the current calendar year’s Budget, so its as-of date is today. Polish UI copy: „Postęp roku”; on Dashboard the Switch „Postęp roku” in the budget panel shows or hides the mark on the meters that may show it, remembered in the browser (ADR-0045); it is not a measure in the metrics panel.
_Avoid_: Month Remaining, Year Pace, tempo roku, using percent complete for this mark

**Month Plan**:
The plan of one calendar month of a Budget in execution, beside the annual Current Plan (ADR-0033). Items are the Categories with a Budget Line on that Budget plus one Set-aside. Exists only for the current and future months. Is either a Proposal (not approved; amounts by Rhythm, ADR-0033 and ADR-0066; Set-aside, ADR-0082) or Approved. “The month has a plan” includes a Proposal. From the 3rd day of the current month inclusive, a Month Plan still in Proposal state raises a `warning` Dashboard notice. Polish UI copy: „plan miesiąca”, „propozycja” / „niezatwierdzony”, „zatwierdzony”.
_Avoid_: monthly Budget, second Budget, Month Remaining, Envelope

**Approved Month Plan**:
The amounts of a Month Plan frozen at approval, immutable afterwards (like Initial Plan), except that a Category Merge adds the amounts of two items of one plan (ADR-0078). They do not change when the annual Current Plan changes. Polish UI copy: „plan zatwierdzony”.
_Avoid_: Initial Plan (for this), Baseline

**Current Month Plan**:
The live amounts of a Month Plan after approval, editable during the month (like Current Plan). At the end of the month it is the final plan. In change mode an expense item in the plan whose actual amount in the month exceeds the Current Month Plan starts with the plan equal to the actual amount (ADR-0062); this raise and the „match actual” action do not apply to an Irregular item (ADR-0066). The „match actual” action sets the plan of an expense row (also a Category outside the plan that may join it) to the actual amount, only when that is greater than the entered amount. The user may lower the amount; nothing is saved before „Zapisz zmiany”, and „Anuluj” discards the raises. The Approved Month Plan never changes. Polish UI copy: „plan miesiąca” during the month, „plan końcowy” in Month Summary, „Ustaw plan na wydatki”, „Plan podniesiony do wydatków”.
_Avoid_: „plan bieżący” (that is the copy of the annual Current Plan, `lang/pl/ui.php:121,163`), Current Plan (without „Month”)

**Month Item Kind**:
Historical: a flag on the Budget Line (Bill or Running), replaced by Rhythm (ADR-0066) and removed in S-24 (ADR-0072). The migration mapped Bill to Fixed Charge and Running to Running (or to Even Annual when the Budget Line was One-off, see One-off Category). A new Budget no longer copies it: the Rhythm is the history of the Category. „Rachunek” here is a kind of item, not an Account („rachunek bankowy” in the UI).
_Avoid_: Paced (for Running), One-off (for Bill), Account (for Bill), fixed / variable

**To Spend**:
A signed reading, in Minor units, not stored: income (plan, then the actual amount after the first inflow in the month) − items by Rhythm − Set-aside. Per Rhythm an item counts: Fixed Charge, plan, then the actual amount after the first operation with a positive amount in the month; Running and Even Annual, the greater of plan and actual; Irregular, Irregular Rate + surplus (see Collected). Plan means Current Month Plan, or Proposal before approval. Actual amount means Execution of that Category from operations dated in that month (refunds netted). May be negative and blocks nothing; in a month with a plan, an entry or Import row after which To Spend is negative and lower than before gives a warning without a block; Own Transfer and Capital Movement do not change it. Below zero it raises an `exceeded` Dashboard notice. An item over plan (an expense Category whose actual amount in the month exceeds its plan amount, any size, including plan 0, for Fixed Charge and Running; for Even Annual when Execution of the year exceeds Current Plan; for Irregular when the surplus is > 0) also raises an `exceeded` Dashboard notice. A bill covered by Collected gives no warning on an operation or in Import. The warning checks the month of the operation date and the later months with a plan on which the entry acts through Collected; rows of one Import chain into one reading (ADR-0073). For Fixed Charge and Running, a saved plan raised to the actual amount ends the item-over-plan notice for that item; the overrun then shows only as a rise against the Approved Month Plan (ADR-0062). Polish UI copy: „do wydania”.
_Avoid_: Spendable Liquidity, Month Remaining, Available Limit, Month Result, Pozostało

**Year after the Month**:
A signed reading, in Minor units, not stored: Execution of a Category before the current month plus the amount its month item counts with by the To Spend rule of its Rhythm (plan, then the actual amount after the first positive amount for income and Fixed Charge, the greater of plan and actual for Running and Even Annual); for Irregular, Execution before the month plus the actual amount of the month. It is compared with the Current Plan of that Category; Year Remainder = Current Plan − Year after the Month, and it may be negative. Read only for the current calendar month of a Budget in execution, for income and expense Categories, and summed per section over all rows of the page. Nothing is written, so the annual Current Plan does not change. Polish UI copy: „W roku … z …”, „zostaje”, „Razem w roku”.
_Avoid_: forecast, Year Pace, Available Limit, Month Remaining

**Set-aside**:
One signed amount per Month Plan: positive („odkładam”) lowers To Spend, negative is a Draw („dobieram”) and raises it. Not a Category, not a transfer, does not change Liquidity. Set-aside has an Approved and a Current amount like a Category amount, and a Proposal writes the Default Set-aside Percent of its planned income when that percent is set (ADR-0082), and otherwise copies a positive Set-aside from the source of the Proposal rule (ADR-0033), while a Draw or no source gives 0. A Draw whose absolute value exceeds Spendable Liquidity (|Draw| > Spendable Liquidity) gives a warning without a block. Polish UI copy: „odkładam / dobieram”.
_Avoid_: Savings (as Category or Account), Reserve, Earmark, transfer, Irregular Rate, Collected

**Default Set-aside Percent**:
A setting of the User: a whole number from 0 to 100, or none (ADR-0082). When set to p, a Proposal writes as its Set-aside p % of the sum of the planned income of that Proposal, rounded down to a whole złoty (in Minor units, intdiv(P × p, 10000) × 100); with no percent the Proposal copies the positive Set-aside of its source (ADR-0033). It acts only on a Proposal: an Approved Month Plan, a Current Month Plan and an amount typed by hand do not change, and the To Spend formula and the rule of Set-aside and Draw stay as they are. It is not recomputed while the User edits incomes in the form. Polish UI copy: „domyślne odkładam”, „% planowanych przychodów”.
_Avoid_: Savings rate, Reserve, Irregular Rate

**Irregular Rate**:
The amount of an Irregular item in the Month Plan of a month (ADR-0066). A Proposal writes floor(Current Plan / 12) of its Budget Line, December takes the remainder (Current Plan − 11 × floor). Approving freezes it in the Approved Month Plan; in change mode it is corrected like any amount (Current Month Plan). In a month without an approved plan, past or current, it is the Proposal formula from the present Current Plan (a past month has no Proposal reading), so a later plan change also moves the rate of past unapproved months and Collected at the start of the following months. 0 in a month in which the year’s Budget is not in execution when Collected is read (a draft Budget, a year without a Budget), where the Category has no Budget Line in that Budget, in an approved month that has no item for the Category until the user adds it in „Zmień plan”, and in months without an approved plan that start after the Budget Line was archived (read when Collected is read, so restoring gives them their rate back) (ADR-0073). Polish UI copy: „stawka”.
_Avoid_: Set-aside, „odkładam”, Fund, Savings, Accrual, Envelope

**Collected**:
A reading, never stored, of the running total of an Irregular Category (ADR-0066). Collected at the start of the month of marking is Opening Collected; Collected at the end of a month m is max(0, Collected at the start of the month + Irregular Rate − actual amount), where the actual amount is the Execution of the Category from operations dated in the month (refunds net), and the end of m is the start of m + 1. The surplus of m is max(0, actual amount − (Collected at the start of the month + Irregular Rate)); it lowers To Spend of its own month. The floor at 0 applies in every month. A late operation or a correction recomputes it. It passes through 31 December without a step: Collected on 1 January is Collected at the end of December. The Budget screen shows it as „zebrane startowe” (ADR-0076). It runs inside a period, from the entry that marked the Category Irregular to the next entry; a month before the first entry of a Category created as Irregular reads as Irregular with Collected 0 and its bills stay out of the chain. A year without a Budget or a Budget not in execution has rate 0, so Collected stands (ADR-0073). Removing „nieregularna” ends it; marking again starts anew. A Category Merge into an Irregular target starts a new period from the current month with the sum of the Collected of both Categories on its first day; into a target that is not Irregular, the Collected of the source disappears (ADR-0078). Does not change Account balances, Liquidity, Execution or Month Result; not a transfer or an account. Polish UI copy: „zebrane”.
_Avoid_: Fund, Sinking fund, Savings, Reserve, Earmark, Set-aside, balance, Account

**Opening Collected**:
The amount typed when a Category is marked Irregular (≥ 0, default 0): Collected at the start of the month of marking. It is the column `opening_collected_minor_units` of the Rhythm entry that starts a period, and it can be corrected on that entry, the one in force in the current month, without changing the month of marking; the chain recomputes itself (ADR-0073). A Category Merge starts a new period whose Opening Collected is the sum of both Collected readings (ADR-0078). „Zebrane startowe” on the Budget screen is not this amount but the Collected on 1 January of the Budget year (the Collected at the end of December of the year before): shown in every Budget, draft or in execution, on the rows whose January lies in an Irregular period (0,00 is shown, no period shows nothing), and marked „prognoza” while the Budget year is later than the year of today (ADR-0076). Polish UI copy: „zebrane startowe”.
_Avoid_: Fund balance, Reserve, Earmark

**Month Pace**:
A percentage: the sum of actual amounts (Execution alone in the month, not the greater of plan and actual) over the sum of plan amounts of the pool of items with Rhythm Running. No reading when the plan sum of the pool is 0. If Month Pace − Month Elapsed is at least 10 percentage points, a `warning` Dashboard notice is raised. Polish UI copy: „tempo”.
_Avoid_: Year Elapsed, Year Pace, Month Remaining

**Month Elapsed**:
The day of the month of the as-of date over the number of days in that month. The as-of date is today for the current month, the last day for a past month, and the first day for a future month (as Year Elapsed). Not an amount. Polish UI copy: „upływ dni”.
_Avoid_: Year Elapsed, Month Pace

**Month Summary**:
A reading of a month from its Month Plan: Approved and Current (final) Month Plan, Execution, deviations, intended Set-aside and the real set-aside (Month Result), drawn amounts (Draw) also over the year. Settling is only a summary: no carry-over and no closing. Read live, only for past months with an Approved Month Plan. Deviation of totals = Execution − Current Month Plan, taken from plain sums (an unpaid Fixed Charge counts as minus its plan). The deviation of an Irregular row is its surplus; the row also shows the Irregular Rate and Collected at the end of the month (ADR-0066). Set-aside difference = Month Result − Current Set-aside. Draw over the year = sum of |Current Set-aside| of the negative ones across Approved Month Plans from January through the summarised month. Polish UI copy: „podsumowanie miesiąca”, „dobrane od początku roku”.
_Avoid_: Month close, Year Close, carry-over

**Planned Balance**:
Income Current Plan minus expense Current Plan for one Budget. A plan-plane summary, not cash on Accounts.
_Avoid_: Balance (unqualified), Liquidity, Account balance, Available Limit, Month Result. Polish UI copy: „Bilans”.

**Month Result**:
Execution income minus Execution expense for one calendar month of a Budget year. Booked Financial Operations dated in that month only, all income and expense Categories, archived ones and every Rhythm included (an Irregular Category counts its actual bill). Expense refunds net into the expense figure. Not Planned Balance, not Month Remaining, not unpaid Cash Expectations, not Own Transfer, not Capital Movement. At the end of a month it is the real set-aside shown in Month Summary.
_Avoid_: Balance (unqualified), Planned Balance, Available Limit, Month Remaining, Liquidity, Year Result, To Spend. Polish UI copy: „Wynik”.

**Year Result**:
Execution income minus Execution expense for one Budget year. Same inclusions as Month Result, for every date in that year rather than one month. A reading; Year Close may post it into Reserve. Polish UI for this reading: „Wynik roku”; the User’s „zysk” / „ostateczny zysk” means this reading, not Planned Balance. Leftover Available Limit is not added on top of it. At Year Close, Earmarks from positive leftovers total at most max(0, this reading); if it is ≤ 0 there are no Earmarks. Not Planned Balance, not a change in Spendable Liquidity or Total funds.
_Avoid_: Month Result (for this year figure), bare Wynik, Bilans, Planned Balance, leftover Available Limit (as if it were this reading or a second pot), Pozostało, profit, surplus (unqualified), Δ Liquidity, Spendable Liquidity, Reserve (as if this reading were the stock)

**Period Report**:
A reading of one Budget for one calendar month or for the whole year, or of the last twelve closed months across Budgets (ADR-0070, ADR-0081). Amounts are Execution: Financial Operations dated in the period, refunds netted, without Own Transfer and Capital Movement. Its result is Month Result for a month and Year Result for a year. Rows are Categories whose Budget Line has at least one Financial Operation in the period (also when the net is 0, also archived ones), grouped by Category Group; a group has a row when it has at least one such Category and sums all of them, archived included. Order is the order of the Category list (ADR-0069), never by amount. Expense rows (groups and Categories) carry a share: tenths of a percent of the period's expense total, rounded half away from zero, negative for a net refund, empty when that total is not positive; income rows have no share. Year over year (ADR-0074): when the User has a Budget of year N − 1 in execution, the report of year N shows beside every amount the same Category’s amount in the equivalent period of N − 1, joined by the list Category id, and the difference (current minus previous, no operations count as 0; a lower expense, a higher income or a higher result is favourable). A period in progress is compared with the same stretch of the previous year, from the start of the equivalent to today’s day one year earlier (29 February gives 28 February); a finished or future period with the whole equivalent. A Category has a row when it has an operation in the current period or in the compared range; the side without operations shows „—”. The share stays the share of the current period. Without a Budget of N − 1 in execution there is no comparison. Polish UI copy: „Raport”; the comparison column is „Różnica”, the year is the column header.

Last twelve months (ADR-0081): a Period Report of the 12 closed calendar months ending with the month before today (UTC), independent of the selected Budget year (on 12 November 2026 that is November 2025 to October 2026). Only months whose Budget is in execution are counted; the months without one are named and neither counted nor estimated, and with no covered month there is no report. Its result is the sum of Month Result of the covered months, to the grosz; polish UI copy „Wynik 12 miesięcy”, never Year Result. Rows are joined across the covering Budgets by list Category id, order and groups as in the year report, the archived mark follows the Category's line in the newest covering Budget, or the list Category when that Budget has no line of it. The share is that of the window's expenses. It has no comparison and no Trend. Polish UI copy: segment „12 miesięcy”, title „Raport: ostatnie 12 miesięcy”.
_Avoid_: rok kroczący, Year Result (for the twelve-month figure), average month

Trend (ADR-0077): a reading of the same Budget year spread over its 12 calendar months, always 12 columns also for months to come. It has the same rows and the same order as the year report without a comparison, and the amount of a row for the year equals its amount there to the grosz. A Category cell is „—” (no value) when the Category has no Financial Operation in that month and 0 when it has some and the net is 0; a group cell is „—” when none of its rows has an operation in that month, otherwise the sum of its rows. The measures of a month are income, expense and result: income is „—” without an income operation, expense without an expense operation, the result only when the month has no operation at all; in other months a missing side counts as 0, and the three equal Month Result for that month. The month of today is marked „w toku” when the Budget year is the current one. The Trend is shown in whole złoty (each cell rounds its own amount half away from zero, the „Razem” column rounds the exact sum, never the sum of rounded cells; the data stay in minor units); this is the only view with amounts rounded for display. Polish UI copy: „Trend”.
_Avoid_: Period (for a Budget), income share, sort by amount, a separate archived section, zmiana % (percent change year over year), joining years by Category name, a compare toggle, a monthly average column, a previous year inside the Trend

**Year Close**:
A User-triggered ritual on a Budget in execution. It computes Year Result, lets the User pick Earmarks from positive leftover Available Limit on any expense Category (sum at most max(0, Year Result); remainder unlabeled), posts Year Result into Reserve, then Aligns every expense Budget Line of that Budget. A later Year Close on the same Budget replaces that Budget’s previous post. It does not lock the year and does not move Liquidity. Not Align by itself. Polish UI copy: noun „Zamknięcie roku”, verb „Zamknij rok”.
_Avoid_: Align, Zakończ, Zakończenie roku, month close, period lock, year-end freeze, closing the books, blokada roku, zamknięcie ksiąg (as if they were this ritual), Month Remaining close

**Reserve**:
A plan-plane stock the User owns, independent of any Budget year and of Account and Term Deposit balances. Holds a signed unlabeled remainder plus Earmarks. Posted from Year Result at Year Close; leftover Available Limit is not added on top. A later negative Year Result reduces unlabeled remainder first (that remainder may go negative) and does not silently consume Earmarks. The User may opt in to add a positive unlabeled amount or an Earmark (in full or in part) into the expense Current Plan of a Budget Line on a later Budget (draft or execution; not the Budget just closed). An Earmark may be released back into unlabeled remainder without touching a Budget. Does not move Liquidity. Polish UI copy: „Rezerwa”.
_Avoid_: Account, Term Deposit, a Category named Reserve, Available Limit (as this stock), Year Result (as if the reading were this stock)

**Earmark**:
A labeled slice of Reserve: an amount in Minor units keyed by an expense Category. Not a Category and not leftover Available Limit after posting. Any expense Category with positive leftover may be a candidate. It leaves Reserve when the User opts in to add some or all of it to a later Budget’s expense Current Plan (default: the same Category; the User may pick another expense Category on that Budget).
_Avoid_: Category, Available Limit, Reserve (unqualified, for this slice), kategoria rezerw, rezerwa kategorii

**Financial Operation**:
An income, expense, or expense refund: dated (past dates included; not a second type), non-zero amount, description, optional Counterparty, one Account, one Category of matching type; the Budget of the year of its date must be in execution, and the Category joins it as a Budget Line. Affects both Account balance and Category Execution. May carry a Raw Statement Description when created by Import. Import-derived description and Counterparty omit payment-instrument numbers from the file row (Polish NRB, IBAN, card PAN including masked); name, merchant, city, amount, and date in that text stay. May settle a Cash Expectation (spawned amount may differ: correction): that Cash Expectation is destroyed and is not a second movement. Interest credited to an Account is Income on that Account and an income Category; it does not name a Term Deposit and is not a Capital Movement.
_Avoid_: Transaction (overloaded with DB transactions), Payment, Transfer (for this type), Term Deposit (as the Account), Capital Movement, Term Deposit interest (as a fourth persisted type), Cash Expectation, archival data / historical dump as a persisted type, Archive (for booked history)

**Expense refund**:
A negative amount on an expense Category (Financial Operation or Cash Expectation), not Income and not a fourth persisted type. As a Financial Operation it restores Account balance and Available Limit, so Execution is net spend; as a Cash Expectation it does not move Liquidity, Execution, or Available Limit.
_Avoid_: Income (for money returned from a purchase), refund as a fourth persisted type

**Cash Expectation**:
A known income, expense, or expense refund that is not yet a Financial Operation: date (future, today, or already overdue; not before the Account opening date), non-zero amount, description, Account, Category of matching type, optional Counterparty. Overdue after create is derived (date before today), not a stored status. The date’s calendar year must have a Budget (draft or execution; a year with no Budget is invalid), and the Category joins it as a Budget Line. One record is one movement, not a series; it sits on Agenda and does not move Liquidity, Execution, Available Limit, or Planned Balance. While unpaid the User may edit (same fields and validation as create, including income ↔ expense refund, not ordinary expense ↔ income) or delete; delete does not move Liquidity or Execution. There is no cancel or skip state and no Financial Operation link. Settlement leaves exactly one Financial Operation (a new one spawned from this record, or an existing one that already is that movement); this record is destroyed, not converted in place and not kept for unpay. A spawned amount that differs from this record is a correction, not a remaining balance: this record still dies in full; Liquidity and Execution move only by that Financial Operation’s amount; nothing is auto-created for a leftover. Attach and Import still require a Settlement candidate (exact amount); to attach a different-amount Financial Operation the User edits this record to that amount first. A leftover still-expected amount is a new Cash Expectation. Edit or delete of that Financial Operation does not recreate a Cash Expectation. An Import preview row or an already-saved Financial Operation is offered for that attach or opt-in only when it is a Settlement candidate.
_Avoid_: Obligation, Payment, Transaction, Unpaid, Pending, Schedule, Scheduled, Plan, Planned, Forecast, Future Operation, Upcoming, Accrual, Due, due date, Payable, Receivable, Reminder, Commitment, Movement (as this record), Operation (as this record), operation date (for this record’s date), Event (as this record), Cash Event, Expectation (unqualified), Financial Operation, Own Transfer, Capital Movement, status (on this record), unpay, skip, cancel (as a persisted state), partial pay (as a verb), remaining balance (on this record), installment

**Counterparty**:
The other party of a Financial Operation or a Cash Expectation: the payer on income, the payee on expense or expense refund. Optional. Absent on Own Transfer. An Import-derived Counterparty omits payment-instrument numbers; if nothing else remains, it is absent.
_Avoid_: Subject, Payee, Payer, Party (as this field’s name)

**Raw Statement Description**:
Immutable text from the Import file row that created a Financial Operation, a Capital Movement, or an Import-born Account on an Own Transfer. Keeps payment-instrument numbers even when Import omits them from description and Counterparty. Null when that record or leg was created by hand; an Own Transfer may hold two after a cross-Import pair. A Capital Movement holds at most one (the liquid Account). Not edited when description or Counterparty later change.
_Avoid_: raw_description, statement_description, Subject

**Own Transfer**:
A move of money between two of the User’s Accounts. No Category. Does not affect Execution. ATM withdrawal is an Own Transfer from bank to cash. An Import row becomes this record when the User classifies it and picks the other Account; both Accounts move on that commit — a later file is not required for it to exist. Cash is an Account like any other for a cross-Import pair. May make the source Account’s current balance negative. May carry a Raw Statement Description per Import-born Account (none, one, or two). A cross-Import pair leaves this single record: it replaces a first-leg Financial Operation and does not move balances twice.
_Avoid_: Internal transfer, Move (as the type name), one-sided Own Transfer, pair (as a record type), Term Deposit (as an endpoint), Capital Movement

**Capital Movement**:
A move of capital between one of the User’s liquid Accounts and one Term Deposit. Not a Financial Operation and not an Own Transfer. No Category. Does not affect Execution. One record changes both holdings by the same positive, non-zero amount, either Account → Term Deposit or Term Deposit → Account. Both endpoints must already exist; cash is a valid Account. A Term Deposit may have many Capital Movements; each names its own Account, so return need not use the same Account as open. Date cannot be in the future, before the Account opening date, or before the Term Deposit opening date. Hand-entry and Import create this type. Import direction follows the signed amount on the Account (debit is Account → Term Deposit; credit is Term Deposit → Account); the User picks an existing Term Deposit, not a Category. May carry one Raw Statement Description when Import-born on that Account. Create, edit, or delete may make the Account and/or the Term Deposit current balance negative; that is Negative Balance Warning, not a block.
_Avoid_: Own Transfer (for this), Transfer, Allocation, Term Deposit transfer, open and return as separate record types

**Negative Balance Warning**:
A non-blocking notice before saving a Financial Operation, Own Transfer, Capital Movement, or a Term Deposit opening snapshot that would make an Account’s or a Term Deposit’s current balance negative. Distinct from exceeding an Available Limit. A Capital Movement that would take both sides below zero shows two notices, one per side. Opening-snapshot save does not change an Account, so it is one Term Deposit notice. After save, every surface that shows that holding’s current balance marks the negative figure (same idea as an Account).
_Avoid_: Overdraft, insufficient funds (both imply a block), a second warning type for Term Deposit

**Liquidity**:
The plane of the User’s holdings as money, distinct from Budget Execution and from Agenda. Not a single unqualified total: the figures on this plane are Spendable Liquidity and Total funds.
_Avoid_: Net worth, Wealth, Portfolio, using Liquidity as one number without Spendable Liquidity or Total funds, Agenda (as this plane)

**Spendable Liquidity**:
The sum of Account current balances (bank and cash). Money not in Term Deposits. Does not include Term Deposit balances.
_Avoid_: Available Limit, Total funds, Liquidity (unqualified), Łączne środki (for this number), To Spend

**Total funds**:
Spendable Liquidity plus the current balances of Term Deposits. A Capital Movement into a Term Deposit does not change Total funds; it moves money from Spendable Liquidity onto that Term Deposit.
_Avoid_: Liquidity (unqualified), Spendable Liquidity, Net worth, Wealth

**Agenda**:
The plane of Cash Expectations that are not yet a Financial Operation, beside Liquidity and Execution. A calendar of those records, not a total and not a liquidity forecast. After settlement or delete the Cash Expectation is not on this plane. There is no cancelled or paid list.
_Avoid_: Horizon, Outlook, Calendar, Schedule, Forecast, Pipeline, Pending, Expectations (as the plane name), Liquidity (for this plane), Execution (for this plane), unpay

**Minor unit**:
An integer count of a currency’s ISO 4217 subunit. Every stored amount is a signed bigint of Minor units, never a decimal of major units. MVP amounts are PLN (scale 2); other currencies are not in the schema yet, so column and code names stay currency-neutral (`*_minor_units`).
_Avoid_: grosze (as a column or code name), cents (as a column name), złotówki in storage, float amounts

**First Run**:
Whether the User has the four start conditions: at least one Account, one Budget, one Category, and one Budget in Execution. Scoped to the User, not to a Budget year. A Cash Expectation and a Term Deposit are not start conditions. The dashboard checklist is the UI for this state (those four rows only); the User may hide that checklist while all four hold.
_Avoid_: Onboarding, wizard, tutorial, First Steps (as a code name; UI copy may say „Pierwsze kroki”)

**Import**:
The controlled creation of Financial Operations, Own Transfers, and Capital Movements from rows in a CSV or XLSX file, after the User chooses an Import format, maps or confirms columns, and reviews a preview. Not a live connection to a bank. Every Import format omits payment-instrument numbers from the live description and Counterparty at parse (Polish NRB, IBAN, card PAN including masked; leftover spaces and blank lines collapse). If description is then empty, it takes the remaining Counterparty; if both are empty the row is invalid, same as a missing description today. Already-committed Import-born live description and Counterparty are brought in line with that omit; Raw Statement Description is not. Hand-entered description and Counterparty are not omitted. Raw Statement Description stays the file row. Preview default type follows the signed amount (credit is Income, debit is Expense); the User must switch type to Capital Movement. One preview row that is capital return plus interest may commit two records in that classification: a Capital Movement (Term Deposit → Account) for the remainder and a Financial Operation (Income) for the interest the User types (and an income Category); those two are not a persisted pair. Hand-entry never creates both from one save. Import does not create a Term Deposit or a Cash Expectation. A preview row may settle an existing Cash Expectation when the User opts in on a Settlement candidate; that commit leaves one Financial Operation, not two.
_Avoid_: CSV Import (as the name of the whole flow), Statement Import, Bank Import, Integration, combined maturity (as a record type)

**Import format**:
A named layout of a bank export file: file type, where the header row is, and which columns are date, description, amount, and optionally Counterparty. MVP ships mBank (CSV), BNP Paribas (XLSX), and Other (manual CSV mapping).
_Avoid_: Integration, Connector, Bank integration, Parser profile, Preset (in domain language; UI may say „format”)

**Import duplicate**:
A preview row that is the same Import row as an already-committed Import-born Financial Operation, Own Transfer, or Capital Movement: same User, Account, date, signed file amount, and Raw Statement Description, frozen at that commit. An Own Transfer may hold two frozen identities, one per Import-born Account, so a later preview row matching either leg is an Import duplicate. A Capital Movement holds one identity, on its liquid Account. When one preview row commits a Capital Movement and an Income Financial Operation, both records receive that same file-row identity (not two identities of the split amounts). Hand-entered records (null Raw Statement Description) are never Import duplicates. `user:restore` keeps the frozen identity and computes its digest again for the restored User and Account (ADR-0080), so „frozen at that commit” does not forbid the recomputation.
_Avoid_: Transaction duplicate, bank transaction id

**Import pair candidate**:
A later Import row that is the other Account’s view of an existing Import-born Financial Operation or Own Transfer. Distinct from Import duplicate (same Account, same frozen row) and from a Settlement candidate. Not a persisted record type; after the User opts in, commit leaves one Own Transfer. A Capital Movement is never an Import pair candidate and never a first-leg. A selected pair and a settlement opt-in cannot both apply to the same preview row.
_Avoid_: Transaction match, transfer duplicate, link, pair (as a record type)

**Settlement candidate**:
An unpaid Cash Expectation offered on Import preview, or an existing Financial Operation offered when attaching from a Cash Expectation: same User, same Account, same type family (income / expense / expense refund), exact type-aware amount in Minor units, and dates within 31 inclusive calendar days. Import preview uses this Account, file date, signed file amount, and current row type; attach uses the Financial Operation’s live fields. The User opts in; the predicate returns a set; a pick consumes that Cash Expectation in this preview. Description, Counterparty, and Category do not gate candidacy. Own Transfer and Capital Movement never qualify.
_Avoid_: auto-match, suggested pair, similar transaction, Import pair candidate, Import duplicate

**Import category suggestion**:
A Category preselected on an Import preview row, read when the preview is built and never stored; it is computed separately for Income and for Expense (an expense refund uses Expense). It learns only from this User's Financial Operations that came from an Import (non-empty Raw Statement Description): a word weighs less the more distinct Categories it appeared under. The target is the winning Category, if it is active and the year of the row's date has a Budget in execution. It never changes the row's type or selection and writes nothing; the User can change it.
_Avoid_: auto-categorisation, AI categorisation, category mapping (bank), category match, similar transaction
