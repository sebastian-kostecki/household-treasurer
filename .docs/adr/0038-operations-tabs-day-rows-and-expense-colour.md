# Operations in tabs, grouped by day, with an expense in ink

The Operations view follows points 7 and 8 of the "C · Atrament i morski" system README (artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/9gJSdtfpFgUAa8oEMgR4HA) that ADR-0035 left out of scope. An operation amount is `+` in `success` for income and a refund and `−` in `foreground` for an expense; `destructive` stays for overruns, negative readings and delete actions, so an expense is no longer a status. The type tile follows the same roles: expense `muted`, income `accent` with `success`, refund `warning-soft` with `refund`, own transfer `transfer`. The financial operations list is grouped by day: a day row carries the date in words and the day sum in the amount column. The day sum is income minus expense with refunds net, without own transfers and capital movements, under the active filters, and the server computes it for the whole day, also when the day runs past the page boundary. Its sign and colour follow the operation amount (`success` above zero, `foreground` otherwise), not `amountToneClass`, because a day of expenses is not an overrun.

The three sections are tabs (`SegmentedControl` neutral, each with a count) instead of three stacked lists. The chosen section lives in `?section=` and survives pagination, filter changes and a refresh; an unknown value is a validation error, like the other filters. A section silenced by a filter stays selected, shows count 0 and a note. The title row has one primary action, "Dodaj operację", which opens a menu (Dochód, Wydatek, Transfer własny, Ruch kapitału; a disabled item shows its reason as text in the item), and "Importuj" is outlined. Financial operations have the columns Opis | Kategoria | Data | Kwota; own transfers and capital movements keep their columns and have no day rows. This reverses the acceptance criteria of issues #19, #35 and #80 (three stacked lists, four separate add controls, red expenses); no earlier ADR held them.

This settles points 7 and 8 that ADR-0035 deferred, and it needs no `CONTEXT.md` entry because it changes no rule and no _Avoid_ term. We rejected one dialog for income and expense with a type choice (the add dialogs stay as they are), a day sum computed on the client from the rows of the page (it would miss the rest of a day that crosses a page boundary and would differ from the server under filters), and `amountToneClass` for the day sum (a negative day would turn red without being an overrun).
