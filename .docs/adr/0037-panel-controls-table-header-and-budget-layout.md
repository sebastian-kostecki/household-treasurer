# Panel recipe, 32px controls, table-header and a Budget without month readings

The panel and control recipe follows points 3 and 5 of the "C · Atrament i morski" system README (artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/9gJSdtfpFgUAa8oEMgR4HA), applied to the whole app. A panel is `card` with a full `border` and `radius-lg` (10px) and no resting shadow, instead of `rounded-2xl` with `border/60`. Controls share one height: `--control-height` is 32px for Button `default` and `icon`, Input, SelectTrigger and `.native-select`, `--control-height-sm` is 26px for Button `sm` and `icon-sm`, and their corners are `radius-md`. Table rows use `--row-height` (34px). The column caption recipe `column-caption` (11px capitals) is replaced by `table-header` (Inter 600, 12/16, normal case, one line, `muted-foreground`); capitals (`label-caps`) stay only above metrics and in the sidebar. The typography styles `page-title`, `section-title`, `table-header`, `label-caps`, `meta`, `kpi-lg`, `kpi`, `amount` and `amount-sum` are named styles in `app.css`; amounts in tables are JetBrains Mono without the unit (`formatMoneyAmount`), and the panel footer says "Kwoty w zł".

The Budget page shows no month readings: Month Result, Month Remaining and To Spend leave it. The Period Report (S-11), the Dashboard and the Month Plan own them, and until S-11 lands a Month Result for a month without an approved Month Plan has no surface in the UI. The page keeps the annual plan, the execution and the year progress. A category execution bar (`ExecutionMeter`, shared with the Dashboard) has two states: `foreground` within the plan and `destructive` when `is_over_limit` is true, with a "przekroczono" Badge `destructive-soft` next to the name of an expense category. The mark of the elapsed year uses `brand-text` and follows `showsYearElapsedMark`. There is no "close to the limit" state, because `CONTEXT.md` defines no threshold for it. Archived categories stay in their own section and render in `muted-foreground` with a `neutral` Badge, and "Pokaż zarchiwizowane" is a Switch bound with `v-model` because the setting is saved at once. Badge gains the variants `ok`, `brand` and `neutral`.

This supersedes in part ADR-0003 (the `rounded-2xl` card with `border/60` surface recipe) and ADR-0033 (the Month Result, Month Remaining and To Spend readings on the Budget page), and it settles points 3 and 5 that ADR-0035 left out of scope. FR-REP-13 and FR-REP-14 in `.docs/prd.md` no longer promise a reading on the Budget page. We rejected keeping the month strip on the Budget page (it duplicates the Dashboard and the Month Plan and needs three queries on each visit), moving Month Result into the Month Plan (it is defined for months without a plan too), a bar with no word at an overrun (colour alone does not carry the status), changing only the buttons and not the fields (controls in one row would differ in height), and a `radius-xl` Dialog in this change (out of scope, the Dialog keeps its radius).
