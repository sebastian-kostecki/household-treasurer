# Operations list without column headers, with "zł" in each amount and coloured type tiles

The Operations list now follows the clickable mockup of "C · Atrament i morski" (`preview.html`) more closely than ADR-0038 did. The panel is `ListSurface` `flush`: the filter bar sits in a bordered strip at the top, and the day rows and rows reach the edges of the card and carry their own horizontal padding. There is no column header row in any section, since the day row carries the date in words, the category is a chip and the amount is right-aligned. No section has a date column either: all three are grouped by day, and the day row above already says the date (with the year when it is not the current one). Financial operations keep Opis | Kategoria | Kwota, and their day row carries the day sum from the server; own transfers and capital movements keep Opis | Konta | Kwota, and their day row carries only the date, because a sum of money moved between one's own accounts says nothing about the budget. This is where the list departs from the mockup, which kept a date column. The search field takes the free width of the filter bar, the month presets and "Filtry" stand on its right, active filter chips get their own line below, and the "X z Y" counter is gone, because the tabs already show the count of each section. The tabs are `SegmentedControl` neutral with `segmented-control-tabs`: no border or background around them, the active tab is a card. Row actions (edit, delete) are one "⋯" button with a menu (`OperationRowMenu`), as on the Budget page, and delete still asks for confirmation in `ConfirmActionDialog`.

Amounts in the Operations list carry the unit ("−86,45 zł", also the day sum and the amounts of own transfers and capital movements), and the panel has no "Kwoty w zł" footer; the explanatory footers of own transfers and capital movements stay. ADR-0037 still holds for the other tables (Budget, Month plan), where the unit stays in the footer. The type tile colours carry the type: expense `destructive-soft` with `destructive`, income `success` at 10% with `success`, refund `warning-soft` with `refund`, own transfer `transfer`. The amount colours stay as ADR-0038 set them (an expense amount is `foreground`), so `destructive` on an amount still means only an overrun or a negative reading; the red tile marks the type, not a status.

This supersedes, in ADR-0038, the columns with a header row and a date column, the lists of own transfers and capital movements without day rows, the "X z Y" counter and the expense tile in `muted`, and in ADR-0037 the unit-in-footer rule for the Operations list only. It changes no rule in `CONTEXT.md` and no _Avoid_ term. We rejected a red expense amount (it would make every expense look like an overrun, which is what ADR-0038 removed) and keeping the column headers on wide screens (they repeated what the chip, the day row and the alignment already say).
