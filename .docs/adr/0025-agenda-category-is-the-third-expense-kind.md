# Expense Category is Paced, One-off, or Agenda Category; Cash Expectations name only the last

An expense Category is one of three: Paced (Month Remaining, never a Cash Expectation), One-off (no Month Remaining, never a Cash Expectation), or Agenda Category (Budget month column is that month’s unpaid Cash Expectations plus booked Execution, not Month Remaining). An income Category is ordinary or an Agenda Category. Only an Agenda Category may be named by a Cash Expectation. Agenda Category still has a User-set Current Plan and Available Limit; unpaid Cash Expectations do not earmark (ADR 0018). Copy onto a new Budget copies the Agenda Category, not its Cash Expectations (ADR 0014).

We rejected a flag “uses Agenda” on Paced/One-off, letting Cash Expectations hang off any Category, deriving Current Plan from Cash Expectations, and treating the month column as Month Remaining or as a liquidity forecast.
