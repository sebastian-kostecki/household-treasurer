# Agenda Category month column lists unpaid Cash Expectations and booked operations, not one mashed number

On the Budget month grain, an Agenda Category shows each unpaid Cash Expectation and each Financial Operation whose **own** date falls in the viewed month. Overdue rows do not roll into the current chip; a late settlement with a later Financial Operation date leaves the estimate in the Cash Expectation’s month (gone once destroyed) and the booking in the Financial Operation’s month. A missed Import settlement stays two rows (expected and booked), not a doubled total and not a hidden expectation. Booking on an Agenda Category does not require settlement; create, edit, and settle stay on Agenda (ADR 0019); the Budget list links through. Opt-in Settlement candidate stays (ADR 0020). There is no extra warning when booked amount differs from the estimate (Available Limit and Negative Balance Warning already run). Dashboard month leftover treats an Agenda Category like One-off (no Month Remaining).

We rejected a single summed month figure, hiding Cash Expectations when any Financial Operation exists in the month, rolling unpaid rows into the viewed month, pinning a Financial Operation to a destroyed Cash Expectation’s month, and a second “over the estimate” warning regime.
